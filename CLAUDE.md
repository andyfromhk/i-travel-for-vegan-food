## VeganBuzz client sites

New VeganBuzz client sites start from this starter. If the project contains a
`CLAUDE-BRIEF.md`, either at the root or inside a `*-site/` folder exported from
VeganBuzz Studio, read it before anything else and follow it. It describes the
client's pages, brand, interactions and the work still to do.

Once the brief's setup step is done, the VeganBuzz kit lives in `src/kit/`.
Its conventions are in `src/kit/KIT.md` and apply alongside `LUMOS.md`.

`gsap` and `lenis` are already installed for the kit's scroll animations and
smooth scrolling.
