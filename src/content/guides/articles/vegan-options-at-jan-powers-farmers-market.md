Every Saturday, the Jan Powers Farmers Market comes to the Brisbane Powerhouse in New Farm.

Let me take you there and see what vegan food options you can find there.

## Location

[The Brisbane Powerhouse, 119 Lamington St, New Farm QLD 4005](https://maps.app.goo.gl/7Tq9Wn4Y4gD8rcuu5)

## What to get from Jan Powers Farmers Market

Sweet & Green offers 80-90% vegan pastries like croissants and doughnuts. Just ask them to double check which ones are vegan.
