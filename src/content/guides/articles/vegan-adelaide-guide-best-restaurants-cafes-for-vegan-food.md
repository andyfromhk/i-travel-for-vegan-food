Adelaide, the capital city of South Australia, is a growing hub for some great plant-based eateries if you are there for a short trip. Although I had some underwhelming dishes during my Adelaide trip, there were also some delicious hidden gems for you to check out.

In this foodie guide, I’ve gathered the best places to eat in this beautiful city:

---

## Staazi & Co.

**Vegan** | *Adelaide CBD (East End)*

![](/media/guides/vegan-adelaide-guide-best-restaurants-cafes-for-vegan-food/img-8159.jpg)

Staazi & Co. is a small, family-owned eatery specialising in vegan Greek street food, and it's one of the most loved vegan spots in Adelaide. I tried the 'combination' yiros, which features vegan chicken and lamb. It's delicious with strong flavours and soft, fluffy pita bread.

Besides yiros, the menu also has ABs (hot chips topped with vegan meat and tzatziki), loaded fries and open plates, all with their house-made tzatziki. The shop itself is tiny with only a few seats, but the outdoor seating facing a quiet alleyway is perfect for a relaxed meal. You'll find it on Grenfell Street in the East End. Note that it's closed on Mondays and Tuesdays.

[*Address and Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5ydzDQ4Yard2yn7w7)

### Veggie Vie

**Vegan** | *Adelaide CBD*

![](/media/guides/vegan-adelaide-guide-best-restaurants-cafes-for-vegan-food/veggieviecafe-2331769138-20250612-080922.webp)

*Photo by @[veggieviecafe](https://www.instagram.com/veggieviecafe/)*

Tucked away on McHenry Street just off Gawler Place, Veggie Vie is a fully vegan cafe that has been a favourite in the city since 2015. Its leafy, rustic space is a lovely spot for a relaxed breakfast or lunch, and they also opens for dinner on Friday.

The daytime menu covers cafe classics like croissants, toasties, burgers, bowls and salads, alongside fresh juices and smoothies. Their cabinet is also worth a look. It's one of the places in the city to find pastries from Clara Cakes, an all-vegan bakery in the Adelaide Hills. On dinner nights the focus shifts to pizza and more creative plates, with vegan wine and beer available.

> **Tip**: Veggie Vie is closed on weekends, so plan a weekday visit. Dinner is only on select nights, so check their Instagram before you go. If you're heading to the Hills, they also have a second cafe in Hahndorf.

[*Address and Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tBWaWqnLz1QziQbx6)

## Clara Cakes

**Vegan** | *Lobethal (Adelaide Hills) & cafes around Adelaide*

![](/media/guides/vegan-adelaide-guide-best-restaurants-cafes-for-vegan-food/clara-cake-vegan-adelaide-food-guide.jpg)

The best thing I had on my trip to Adelaide. Clara Cakes is an all-vegan bakery, and I got this croissant from one of the cafes that stock their pastries. Perfect flaky texture, crispy on the ends and airy in the middle. The filling is also generous. I chose the matcha almond flavour, which is sweet with a subtle bitterness from the matcha. A must-try in Adelaide.

Their bakery is on the main street of Lobethal, a small town in the Adelaide Hills. They sell croissants, cruffins, cakes and sourdough. The shop is only open for limited hours and sells out quickly, so check their Instagram before making the trip and get there early. If you can't make it to the Hills, Veggie Vie in the CBD stocks their pastries, and you can also pre-order for pick-up or delivery.

[*Address & Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/fZPV4UjGLUtoDCJJ7)

### K-Pub

**Vegan-options** | *Adelaide CBD*

![](/media/guides/vegan-adelaide-guide-best-restaurants-cafes-for-vegan-food/kpub-adelaide-2809446704-20261001-225810.webp)

*Photo by @[kpub_adelaide](https://www.instagram.com/kpub_adelaide/)*

K-Pub is a casual Korean bistro and bar on Gouger Street. It isn't a vegan restaurant, but it has some great vegan items like Korean fried chicken and bibimbap.

> **Tip**: It's a short walk from the Adelaide Central Market, so it's an easy dinner stop after a day exploring the city.

[*Address & Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/bg5phLQJHuyAVEnYA)

## Metta Sol

**Vegan-options** | *Goodwood*

![](/media/guides/vegan-adelaide-guide-best-restaurants-cafes-for-vegan-food/metta-sol-vegan-adelaide-food-guide.jpg)

*(Metta Sol has changed owners in May 2023 and is now serving meat with vegan options. This review was written prior to that change.)*

[Metta Sol](https://www.mettasol.com.au/) is a modern Vietnamese restaurant with an all vegan menu. I tried the turmeric crepe which has tofu, cauliflower and bean sprouts as the filling, with a big side salad and tangy, slightly spicy sauce.

The crepe is thick and the fillings are rich and flavourful. Even though the price is on the higher side, I’d still recommend checking this place out.

[*Address and Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/qqDk8E9fUtfpCGur7)

## Zenhouse

**Vegan** | *West Hindmarsh*

![](/media/guides/vegan-adelaide-guide-best-restaurants-cafes-for-vegan-food/zenhouse-vegan-adelaide-food-guide.jpg)

Zenhouse is a vegetarian Chinese restaurant that has been serving classic dishes and yum cha in Adelaide since 2006. However, the food was a bit disappointing during my visit. The tofu clay pot that I tried was quite bland, and the Zenhouse fried rice could do with more toppings. On the plus side, they have a wide selection of tea.

Since my visit, Zenhouse has closed its Bent Street restaurant in the city and moved to Port Road in West Hindmarsh, about 10 minutes from the CBD. The new restaurant serves a plant-based menu that mixes yum cha with Asian and Western fusion dishes. It's mainly open for dinner, with lunch on weekends. A few dishes use honey, so double-check with the staff if you're vegan.

[*Address and Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/N7D1VXZ8t3s8jJaD7)

### Allegra Dining Room

**Vegan** | *Adelaide CBD*

![](/media/guides/vegan-adelaide-guide-best-restaurants-cafes-for-vegan-food/allegradiningroom-920396343-20260512-215905.webp)

*Photo by @[*allegradiningroom*](https://www.instagram.com/allegradiningroom/)*

Allegra is Adelaide's plant-based fine diner, an intimate 28-seat upstairs dining room. It's highly regarded for its creative multi-course menus, which change with the seasons and focus on local produce. Past dishes have included zucchini flowers with plant-based ricotta and mint, and Jerusalem artichoke gratin with cashew bechamel.

Drink pairings are available, and the warm, retro-styled room makes it perfect for a special occasion. Allegra now runs in seasonal "series," so it's well worth planning around.

> **Tip**: Booking ahead is a must given the small dining room.

[*Address and Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/rb9u4gx8abHthLAF9)

### Joy of Flora

**Vegan** | *West Croydon*

![](/media/guides/vegan-adelaide-guide-best-restaurants-cafes-for-vegan-food/jof-cafe-870214324-20260716-113008.webp)

*Photo by @[*jof.cafe*](https://www.instagram.com/jof.cafe/)*

Joy of Flora (JOF Cafe) is a fully vegan cafe, art gallery and event space right next to West Croydon train station, an easy trip from the city. It's known for its relaxed, community feel and a hidden "secret garden" out the back that's perfect on a sunny day.

The brunch and lunch menu changes with the seasons, and most of it is made from scratch in-house. Popular picks include the McJOF breakfast muffin, with a sausage patty, melted cheese, vegan egg and hash brown, and the satay tofu bowl. Don't skip their raw cakes and baked goods either.

[*Address and Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/y4hbs2vW4gWzbHFS9)

## Two-Bit Villains

**Vegetarian** | *Adelaide CBD (Rundle Mall)*

![](/media/guides/vegan-adelaide-guide-best-restaurants-cafes-for-vegan-food/two-bit-villains-vegan-adelaide-food-guide.jpg)

Located upstairs in the Adelaide Arcade on Rundle Mall, Two-Bit Villains is a retro soda bar and diner that has been serving vegetarian American-style food since 2011. Everything on the menu is vegetarian, and all the items can be veganised. I liked the Arkan-Sauce fried chicken burger that I got. The fried chicken is crispy with an interesting texture, but it would be even better if the cheese was melted and the burger had more sauce.

The diner sits in the arcade's historic Grand Ballroom, and its balcony overlooking Rundle Mall is a fun spot for people-watching while you sip one of their house-made sodas. Since my visit, Two-Bit Villains has been taken over by new owners, who have kept the menu vegetarian and vegan-friendly. Dairy is the default, so remember to ask for the vegan version when ordering. It's also up a couple of flights of stairs, which may be tricky for some visitors.

[*Address and Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5VqTKmwGyyVBAkFk7)

### Ping An Veggie Time

**Vegan** | *Dover Gardens & Bowden*

![](/media/guides/vegan-adelaide-guide-best-restaurants-cafes-for-vegan-food/plant4bowden-3817671767444583523-2329330811-20260125-161920.webp)

*Photo by @[*pinganveggietime*](https://www.instagram.com/pinganveggietime/)*

Ping An Veggie Time calls itself Adelaide's first 100% vegan Thai restaurant. This family-run business is named after the owners' son and started out in Brisbane before moving to Adelaide. You'll find them at a restaurant in Dover Gardens and a food stall inside Plant 4 Bowden, a lively market shed full of food stalls.

The menu covers Thai favourites like pad thai, pad see ew, homemade curries and laksa. The satay noodles, with Hokkien noodles in a creamy peanut sauce, are a standout. Starters like the taro and sweet potato spring rolls, curry puffs and steamed BBQ buns are also popular. Everything is cooked without onion or garlic.

## 48 Flavours

**Vegetarian** | *Multiple Locations*

![](/media/guides/vegan-adelaide-guide-best-restaurants-cafes-for-vegan-food/48-flavours-vegan-adelaide-food-guide.jpg)

48 Flavours is a great family-owned gelato shop with clearly marked vegan options. There were about 17 vegan flavours when I visited. I tried the Black Magic (charcoal and cocoa) and Blueberry Crumble. Both flavours are rich with amazing texture.

Their vegan gelato is made with oat milk, soy milk or coconut water, and their vegan flavours and sorbets are certified by Vegan Australia, so you can order with confidence. Flavours rotate, so look out for the vegan labels in the cabinet. The Gouger Street store is close to the Central Market and Chinatown and stays open into the evening, making it perfect for dessert after dinner. They also have several other stores around Adelaide.

## Daisy Coffee Shop

**Vegan-option** | *Forestville*

![](/media/guides/vegan-adelaide-guide-best-restaurants-cafes-for-vegan-food/dear-daisy-vegan-adelaide-food-guide.jpg)

While Daisy Coffee Shop (previously known as Dear Daisy) serves meat, they also had some decent vegan options when I visited. The cafe has a beautiful space and friendly staff. I tried the "Vegan Biggie" breakfast and the pumpkin falafel ciabatta. Both dishes were well made and tasty.

Since my visit, the cafe has been taken over by a new owner and renamed Daisy, with a simpler menu that moves away from its vegan-focused roots. Favourites like the tofu scramble and house-made hash browns are still on the menu, but the vegan range is smaller than before, so check what's available on the day. It's open for breakfast and lunch every day, closing at 2pm.

[*Address and Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/wAvTXwinvf1kH4Bf8)

## Cafe Troppo

**Vegan-option** | *Adelaide CBD*

![](/media/guides/vegan-adelaide-guide-best-restaurants-cafes-for-vegan-food/cafe-troppo-vegan-adelaide-food-guide.jpg)

Cafe Troppo has a beautiful rustic atmosphere, but my meal there was quite disappointing. I ordered the Flower Power, a vegan breakfast dish with ingredients like marinated mushrooms, pesto and avocado on sourdough toast. Although the portion size was good, the whole dish was very oily, making it hard to enjoy. They even drizzled extra oil on the plate and on top of all the ingredients.

That said, the cafe's focus on sustainability is impressive. Almost everything is sourced from South Australia, and their own gardener grows organic produce and delivers it by bike. The menu changes with the seasons, and recent vegan options include a vegan toastie and a smoky baked bean bowl. It might be worth another try if you're near Whitmore Square, especially for their coffee, which gets a lot of praise. The kitchen closes at 2pm.

[*Address and Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/UdkEc9EGAP1ZuJDe8)
