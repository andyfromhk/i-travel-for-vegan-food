A quick guide showing you all the vegan options at the domestic terminal at Brisbane Airport. These cafes and restaurants are all located after security screening.

## Qantas Food Court

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2939-jpg.jpeg)

### Roll’d

They provide a few good vegan options like the ‘tofu soldier’ rice paper roll, vegetable spring rolls, mushroom tofu pho and more.

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2953-jpg.jpeg)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2955-jpg.jpeg)

### Ze Pickle

This burger shop has one basic vegan option called 'I’m A Vegan' with patty, guac, tomato and ketchup.

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2942-jpg.jpeg)

### Nippon Ramen

The vegetable ramen in this shop is marked vegan with toppings like bamboo shoots, mushrooms and carrots.

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2945-jpg.jpeg)

### Mi Casa Burritos

The classic Mexican dishes can be made vegan with the ‘vegetarian’ filling which is sautéed mushrooms, and ask them to take away any shredded cheese or sour cream.

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2948-jpg.jpeg)

### YO! Sushi

Some simple options like avocado roll, vegetarian roll and edamame.

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2949-jpg.jpeg)

### Seeds by Bruno Loubet

They have one clearly marked vegan salad - ‘Beetroot balsamic onion & toasted almonds, as well as chia seed cups and smoothies.

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2957-jpg.jpeg)

## Virgin Food Court

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2967-jpg.jpeg)

### Soul Origin

Some salads are clearly marked with the vegan symbol like the ‘tonkatsu plant-based schnitzel, pumpkin couscous and falafel.

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2964-jpg.jpeg)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2965-jpg.jpeg)

### Motto Motto

You’ll find vegan options including two donburi rice bowls - Tofu teriyaki and miso eggplant, a plant based miso ramen, and vegetable & chive gyoza. Keep in mind that the tofu curry rice is not vegan.

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2968-jpg.jpeg)

---

## Central Area

### New Farm Confectionery

Chocolate specialty shop with vegan options marked with a V. The gluten free options are marked as well. Some vegan options I saw include hot chocolate stirrer with raspberry, and raspberry & strawberry sherbet.

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2922-jpg.jpeg)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2921-jpg.jpeg)

### Krispy Kreme

Some vegan option doughnut including the tasty apple custard crumble doughnut. Their classic glazed doughnut is also vegan.

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2962-jpg.jpeg)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2924-jpg.jpeg)

### Bound

This bookstore cafe has a few vegan food options including superfood almond chia pudding and Vegan loaded veggie sourdough sandwich. They also carry the chocolate brand Kokopod here; some of the flavours are vegan like the ‘raspberry fever’.

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2927-jpg.jpeg)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2932-jpg.jpeg)

### The Common

No clearly marked vegan options.

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2926-jpg.jpeg)

### The Aviary

No clearly marked vegan options except fries.

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2937-jpg.jpeg)

### Newstead Brewing Co

No clearly marked vegan options.

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-at-brisbane-airport-domestic-terminal/img-2916-jpg.jpeg)

## Virgin Australia Airline Gates

### Local (Near gate 43-49)

They have a vegan-option harvest bowl by removing feta cheese (no vegan cheese substitute).
