The Sydney Vegan Market, hosted by Vegan NSW, is a vibrant and inclusive space that celebrates everything vegan, and most importantly, you’ll want to try all the food here.

Happening every third Sunday, this vegan market is a paradise for vegan foodies. You’ll find a huge range of food stalls that can leave even the pickiest eaters spoilt for choice.

I love seeing the market being so popular on the day of my visit. From live music to free yoga classes, there's something for everyone in this market.

## Location

Check their Instagram page for the latest location. I've visited them in the Entertainment Quarters and Sydney Olympic Park.
