Let me take you to the 2024 Brisbane Vegan Expo.

It was a 2-day event where vegan vendors and visitors come together to celebrate everything vegan. I visited on the second day and was happy to find a great variety of delicious vegan food. If you plan to visit the next one, bring an empty stomach to try all the vegan food (including all the amazing samples) you can find in this expo.

Other than the vegan food, there are also guest speakers, cooking demos, live music and more.

![](/media/guides/2024-brisbane-vegan-expo/img-1300.webp)

*Cooking demo by Simon Toohey*

## Location

[Exhibition Building, Brisbane Showgrounds, Bowen Hills, QLD 4006, Australia](https://goo.gl/maps/AuYGPaFgF2ZBMrZ36)

## Hours & Date

Saturday, 14 September 2022: 10am to 5pm

Sunday, 15 September 2022: 10am to 4pm

## Entrance Fee

Tickets are $11 for both day access
