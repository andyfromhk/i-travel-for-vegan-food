## **Vegan Options In Southbank**

### Ma Pa Me

![](/media/restaurants/ma-pa-me/img-8362.jpg)

[Ma Pa Me](/restaurants/ma-pa-me) is a southeast Asian restaurant with a vegan menu. During my 3 visits, the food has been consistently delicious. Stand out dishes include the tempura eggplant (crispy with a rich sauce) and gado gado (love the strong peanut flavour).

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/tSgg6pQSJd9QcfgY8)

![Pad Thai](/media/restaurants/ma-pa-me/20230410-131220.jpg)

### Zeus Street Greek

Zeus Street Greek is a restaurant chain specialising in Greek wraps. However, their vegan range has shrunk in recent years. They got rid of the vegan chicken and vegan lamb. The only vegan protein option is falafels.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://g.page/zeus-street-greek-south-bank?share)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-3052-202.jpeg)

*Pythagoras vegan chicken wrap that was previously available*

### Grill’d

Grill'd offers a few vegan burgers using Beyond Meat.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/GwPQWDxnaHJWHC7E6)

![](/media/guides/australia-chain-cafes-restaurants-vegan-options/img-7034.webp)

### Cocobliss

[Cocobliss](/restaurants/coco-bliss) is a health food bar with many vegan options. The Ferrero acai bowl is a healthy tasty choice.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://g.page/cocoblisssouthbank?share)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-4457-202.jpeg)

### San Churro

A dessert shop with vegan churro, ice cream, thick shake and more. Food generally tastes good but this Southbank branch is sometimes not consistent in the food quality.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://g.page/sanchurrosouthbank?share)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-8395.webp)

### Guzman y Gomez

A Mexican chain with dishes that can be made vegan. Sauteed vegetable and shredded mushroom are the two vegan filling. To make it vegan, substitute cheese with vegan chimi mayo for free.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/WViEq91Ge5tu6kaU7)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-3758.jpeg)

*Fable shredded mushroom tacos*

### Happy Pops

Cool down with a gelato pop at Happy Pops. They have some vegan options including hazelnut & chocolate and mango & coconut yoghurt.

**Vegetarian** | [*Address & Hours*](https://g.page/Happypopssouthbank?share)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-7180.jpeg)

*Pistachio vegan pop*

### Nodo

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/gZ1ahkjzmPrmgrH4A)

*They usually carry one vegan doughnut flavour*

This craft beer bar has clearly-marked vegan options, including vegan burgers and tacos.

## Vegan Options In South Brisbane

### Vegeme

[Vegeme](/restaurants/vegeme) is a Taiwanese vegetarian restaurant with many vegan items. Reasonable prices with generous portions. Two dishes here that I love are rice with bean sauce and beancurd wallet & veggies.

**Vegetarian** | [*Address and Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/kWYNLJR1Gp9vW7haA)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-7332.webp)

### Okuman Karaage & Ramen Bar

Although not a veg restaurant, [Okuman](/restaurants/okuman-karaage-ramen-bar) has a relatively big vegan menu. Vegan bento boxes, tempura and ramen are great classic options.

**Vegan-option** | [*Address and Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/iV9HaWGE9fMZv88j8)

![Tempura platter, vegetable croquette curry rice, teriyaki tofu and yakisoba](/media/restaurants/okuman-karaage-ramen-bar/20230401-122147.jpg)

*(From top) tempura platter, vegetable croquette curry rice, teriyaki tofu and yakisoba*

### Fish Bowl

[Fish Bowl](/restaurants/fishbowl) is a healthy fast food chain with a few customisable vegan options like a miso eggplant bowl, and tofu box:

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KG6xGbcp3kcbwJpY7)

![](/media/guides/australia-chain-cafes-restaurants-vegan-options/img-5096.webp)

### Kinara

This Indian restaurant has a section of 8 vegan options. The two curries that I tried (pumpkin masala, and Baigan bharta which was a smoky roasted eggplant curry) were great. Unfortunately, I checked with the staff and they said none of the naan, kulcha or papadums can be made vegan. So I went with roti and rice to go with the curries.

**Vegan-option** | [*Address and Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/hEBfjCLgULvpVQg57)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-5950.webp)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-5963.webp)

### Taro’s Ramen

The vegan menu at [Taro's Ramen](/restaurants/taros-ramen-south-brisbane) include vegan ramen, gyoza and curry rice. The ramen is tasty with a deep-flavoured broth, but they could be a bit more generous with the amount of toppings.

**Vegan-option** | [*Address and Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/Bub8wSfbxKkcATb28)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-3807.jpeg)

*Vegan classic ramen*

### Uncle Don

A donburi serving Japanese rice bowls. They have a few clearly marked vegan options like nasu dengaku don (sweet miso grilled eggplants) and tofu on tofu don (teriyaki fried tofu with tofu cream).

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KG6xGbcp3kcbwJpY7)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-9590-jpg.jpg)

### El Planta

A vegan Mexican restaurant with a seasonal menu. The value is not the best, and the tacos are rather small for the price.

**Vegan** | [*Address & Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/gomkeWAmVoAWCjF8A)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/00f36fe5-f900-4829-8807-1f7a026ef771-10458-000005b55ccf9bf3.jpeg)

### Gelato Messina

[Messina](/restaurants/gelato-messina) has some great vegan options. All their sorbets are vegan, including the delicious pandan+coconut milk and salted coconut+mango salsa. The cone is vegan as well.

**Vegan-options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/ELQFgCmDi2TuRfJJA)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-0495.jpg)

### Punjabi Palace

An Indian restaurant with vegan options. Vegetarian options that are marked dairy free (DF) are vegan.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/jne7zZhPYSLx2HAz6)

## Vegan Options In West End

### Vegan Restaurant West End

[Vegan Restaurant West End](/restaurants/vegan-restaurant-west-end) serves a wide variety of Asian dishes like honey chicken and salty fish fried rice. Food is consistently delicious with reasonable prices.

**Vegan** | [*Address and Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/orh59ZdQXhp8LLZ17)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-3187.jpeg)

### Kurtosh

[Kürtősh](/restaurants/kurtosh) is a traditional Hungarian pastry. It's freshly made with a crunchy caramelised crust and soft bread-y inside. All flavours except the nutella one is vegan. In the cabinet, you'll find vegan choc chip cookie, and two vegan cake slices (peanut butter choc royale, and apple & pecan crumble).

**Vegan-options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/gnLU1K9jNVo7YBxi8)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/kurtosh.jpg)

*Pistachio & almond kurtosh*

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-8918.webp)

### Anita Gelato

[Anita Gelato](/restaurants/anita-gelato) has a several vegan sorbet flavours. The sorbet flavours are vegan and there are sometimes milk-based flavours made with non-dairy milk, including the dark chocolate gelato.

**Vegan-options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/Sfko8bd2T1TV6zA56)

![](/media/restaurants/anita-gelato/img-6585.webp)

### Oakberry

An acai bar with build-your-own bowls and smoothies. Prices are a bit higher but delicious acai bowls. Go for the combination of peanut butter, strawberry and granola if it's your first time here.

**Vegetarian** | [*Address & Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/QbHqfxnPnfHgsXUm9)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-4383.jpeg)

### Wrapture

[Wrapture](/restaurants/wrapture) in West End specialises in wraps. The satay tofu wrap and falafel wrap are great. Keep in mind that you would need to specify vegan when ordering and the vegan wrap is $1 extra.

**Vegan-option** | [*Address and Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/1mYvJUseE7SPegtV8)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-2931.jpeg)

*Falafel and satay tofu wraps*

### The Burrow

[The Burrow](/restaurants/the-burrow) is a cafe with eclectic vibes with pretty good vegan options including pizza, burgers and more.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://g.page/theburrowwestend?share)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-5447.webp)

### Insane Acai Bar

[Insane Acai Bar](/restaurants/insane-acai-bar) with high quality acai and smoothie bowls. At the time of my visit, they also have all vegan desserts and treats in the fridge cabinet.

**Vegetarian** | [*Address & Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/95k7TtjZe92TfaNz7)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-0115.jpg)

### Yochi

A froyo (frozen yoghurt) chain with vegan options. For the yoghurt, you'll find chocolate and acai as vegan flavours. And for toppings, you'll find a variety of fruits, cookie dough, Oreo, biscoff sauce and more.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/3SgdrJzSCt4xiQNg6)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-8224.webp)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-8223.webp)

### Fat Carrot

[Fat Carrot](/restaurants/fat-carrot) is a Vietnamese cafe with a separate vegan menu with plenty of options. Try their vegan pulled pork bahn mi and the tofu rice paper roll if you haven't already.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://g.page/thefatcarrotwestend?share)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/img-7639.webp)

### Mu'ooz

Mu'ooz is a not-for-profit social enterprise and restaurant located in West End. It provides a great way to try some authentic Eritrean cuisine, including enjera, stews and curries. The vegan options are clearly marked. Food is tasty, but some may find the texture lacking a bit with many options being stews.

**Vegan-option** | [*Address and Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/WhoV4WNr2c8FMJdz8)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-west-end-southbank-south-brisbane/muooz.jpg)

### The Indian Kitchen

Affordable Indian place with vegan options like samosa and curry.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/14YTWUt4BubBxh7NA)

### Catchment Brewing

A restaurant and bar on Boundary Street with clearly marked vegan options including pizza with vegan cheese option and plant based cheeseburger.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://g.page/catchmentbrewingco?share)

### Domino’s Pizza

Domino's Pizza has a vegan menu including vegan cheese pizza and cheesy garlic bread. In general it's pretty good and nostalgic. I like the beef & onion pizza the best.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://goo.gl/maps/xBasLo9fCTEk1YEQ8)

### Gala Thai

Thai restaurant on Boundary Street offering some classic Thai dishes like laksa and som tum. Vegan options are clearly marked on the menu.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://g.page/galathaiwestend?share)
