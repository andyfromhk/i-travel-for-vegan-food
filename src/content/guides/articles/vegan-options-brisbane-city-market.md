Every Wednesday, Brisbane City Market comes to Brisbane CBD outside the Treasury Casino.

Sometimes it's hard to find vegan food in a non-vegan market, but there are quite a few options in this market.

Let's look at what you can get here:

## Location

[Reddacliff Place, Queen St, Brisbane City QLD 4000](https://goo.gl/maps/ruzGYQqmYNydteQi8)

## What to get from Brisbane City Market

Your best option here is burgers and loaded fries from Grassfed (if they are attending, check their socials for details). The food is delicious and indulgent.

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-brisbane-city-market/brisbane-city-markets-grassfed-burger.webp)

[Veganyumm](/restaurants/veganyumm) is also a regular at the Brisbane City Markets. They have a great selection of sweet and savoury options. Here I tried the matcha white choc cookie and the biscoff loaded cookie:

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-brisbane-city-market/img-6240.jpg)

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-brisbane-city-market/img-6747.jpg)

Get a refreshing drink from Iced Tea Co

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-brisbane-city-market/img-6743.jpg)

This shop has a vegan gozleme: spinach, tomato, olive and onion:

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-brisbane-city-market/img-6753.jpg)

The Bagel Boys has "The Vegan" bagel which includes roasted capsicum, avo, tomato chilli relish and more on a bagel of your choice:

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-brisbane-city-market/img-9185.webp)

Matcha-specialty shop Emerald Whisk has some vegan-friendly matcha drinks including the strawberry matcha and brown sugar matcha, but the taro matcha can't be made vegan:

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-brisbane-city-market/img-9182.webp)

Okinawa Taco Brothers has vegan options for their taco rice and taco wrap. However, they don't have vegan alternatives for cheese or sour cream.

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-brisbane-city-market/img-8875.webp)

Falafels with samples to try (but be careful as some of their dips are not vegan):

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-brisbane-city-market/img-6751.jpg)

Hummus, falafel pockets and plates:

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-brisbane-city-market/img-6749.jpg)

Brat Haus has vegan sausage available:

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-brisbane-city-market/img-6739.jpg)

Batch cookie bar has vegan option cookies:

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-brisbane-city-market/img-6745.jpg)

Tea Stop is another option for a vegan drink:

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-brisbane-city-market/img-6741.jpg)

Churros Bite makes vegan option churros:

![](/media/guides/vegan-options-brisbane-city-market/img-6756.jpg)
