The Brisbane Vegan Expo in 2022 just finished and it was amazing!

Over 110 vendors and thousands of visitors came together to celebrate everything vegan. Go with an empty stomach because you'll be impressed with the variety of food you'll find here. Or you can choose to go around to try out the free samples before deciding what plant-based products to bring home.

Other than the vegan stalls, there are also guest speakers and programs, including a cooking demonstration from [Izakaya Midori](/restaurants/izakaya-midori) and screening of the movie Slay.

## Location

[Exhibition Building, Brisbane Showgrounds, Bowen Hills, QLD 4006, Australia](https://goo.gl/maps/AuYGPaFgF2ZBMrZ36)
