Gold Coast vegan market is held regularly in multiple locations like Southport and Mudgeeraba. Vegan stalls gather and create an ethical, sustainable and cruelty-free event. And the entry is free.

You can find mouth-watering vegan food, drinks and desserts. You'd probably want to bring a friend to try a bit of everything: from pizza and burgers, to ice cream and cupcakes.

After stuffing your face with food, check out other cruelty-free shops selling vegan leather accessories, candles, crystals and more.

The vibe is generally relaxed and chill and of there is live music from local musician.

It’s also a great way to show your non-vegan friends and family how delicious vegan food is. The stalls change regularly, so you will probably want to visit more than once if you have the chance.
