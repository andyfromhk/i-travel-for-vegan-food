Ever had difficulty finding vegan food in Brisbane CBD?

Among the office buildings and shopping centres, there are some hidden vegan gems that you can go to. From comforting curry to vegan doughnuts, there are choices for everyone.

And if you happen to visit the city on a Wednesday, be sure to check out the [Brisbane City Market](/articles/vegan-options-brisbane-city-market) as well, where you'll likely find vegan burgers from [Grassfed](https://www.instagram.com/grassfedplantbased/) or desserts from [Veganyumm](https://www.instagram.com/veganyumm/).

Here are some of my favourite places to get vegan food in Brisbane CBD:

---

## Vege Rama

![](/media/restaurants/vege-rama/img-7242.webp)

![](/media/restaurants/vege-rama/img-7354.webp)

A vegan eatery near Anzac Square that has a great selection of vegan comfort food. The dishes change daily but you can expect to find curry, dosa, kofta, salad, and desserts. My favourite is the Thai massaman curry and lemongrass soy nuggets. There are some seatings but it can get quite busy during lunch hour.

[**_See restaurant details here →_**](/restaurants/vege-rama)

## Govinda’s

![](/media/guides/vegan-brisbane-city-cbd/69898fbb-9a91-440a-a4eb-77d58d5bfe28-5498-00000285d323c028.jpeg)

*Takeaway curry rice and koftas*

Govinda's is a vegetarian chain specialising in Indian dishes. Choose from one of their 'feast meals' which include curry, rice and koftas. The vegan chocolate peanut butter slab is pretty delicious as well.

[**_See restaurant details here →_**](/restaurants/govindas-brisbane-cbd)

---

## Niko Niko

![](/media/guides/vegan-brisbane-city-cbd/img-3846.webp)

A Japanese takeaway spot on Adelaide Street. The food is pretty tasty with good prices. The vegan options are marked clearly with a VG on the menu. You can expect to find options like veg tempura curry rice and teriyaki tofu donburi. They also have some vegan sushi in the fridge section.

[**_See restaurant details here →_**](/restaurants/niko-niko)

---

## Gelatissimo

![](/media/guides/vegan-brisbane-city-cbd/img-1005.jpeg)

This ice cream shop on Queen Street has vegan options. They usually have some specials like chocolate peanut butter flavour, alongside a good selection of vegan sorbets.

[**_See restaurant details here →_**](/restaurants/gelatissimo)

---

## Felix For Goodness

![](/media/restaurants/felix-for-goodness/img-8271.webp)

Popular CBD alleyway cafe with clearly marked vegan options. The food quality is good despite the relatively high price tag. I tried the vegan tuna bruschetta which is delicious with chickpeas with seaweed as the 'tuna', alongside fried tofu, roasted tomatoes, and standout pickled cucumber. Other options include cafe classics like granola or falafel bowls.

[**_See restaurant details here →_**](/restaurants/felix-for-goodness)

---

## Cheeky Poke

![](/media/guides/vegan-brisbane-city-cbd/img-9646.webp)

![](/media/guides/vegan-brisbane-city-cbd/img-9650.webp)

This poke bowl shop has 2 vegan option for poke bowl - tofu and mushroom. I've tried both of them and they are tasty. You can also find vegan-option tacos. Two of the main protein options are vegan - tofu with miso tahini, and mushroom with beetroot hummus. Make sure to ask them to omit mayo to make it vegan.

Keep in mind that this place also serve meat and seafood. And the smell of seafood inside the restaurant could be quite strong.

[**_See restaurant details here →_**](/restaurants/cheeky-poke)

![](/media/guides/vegan-brisbane-city-cbd/img-8249.webp)

## Vapiano

![](/media/guides/vegan-brisbane-city-cbd/img-0634-jpg.webp)

Vapiano is a Italian eatery in Brisbane CBD offering vegan-friendly pasta, pizza and more. For some pasta like the Fusilli crema di funghi, you can change the sauce to a vegan-friendly one using dairy-free lentil cream. And they have vegan cheese for pizzas, though the cheese is not very melty.

Overall the taste is decent, but the quality is a bit below my expectations for the price for the non-melty vegan cheese and cream pasta that lacks sauce.

[**_See restaurant details here →_**](/restaurants/vapiano)

## Krispy Kreme

![](/media/guides/vegan-brisbane-city-cbd/img-8891.webp)

Australian doughnut chain with clearly marked vegan options like apple custard crumble and cinnamon doughnut. They may also have limited time specials that are vegan. Some of the options that options that are not marked 'vegan' are actually vegan-friendly, like the Kookies 'n' Kreme. [Check this FAQ page](https://www.krispykreme.com.au/blog/vegan-doughnut-range/) for their latest range.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/rv5ArtVGHoSR67fcA)

## Greenstreat

![](/media/guides/vegan-brisbane-city-cbd/img-9721.webp)

Despite being a healthy salad bowl shop, they only have one default vegan option which is the Spicy Harvest Bowl, which has roasted tofu, warm shrooms, roasted sweet potato, spicy broccoli, avo and more.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://maps.app.goo.gl/qpN2ivMA6zvcYMZU9)

## Comuna Cantina

A Latin street food restaurant with a vegan menu. Choices include burrito, taco and nacho.

## Pancake Manor

![](/media/guides/vegan-brisbane-city-cbd/img-3946.webp)

![](/media/guides/vegan-brisbane-city-cbd/img-3963.webp)

They have vegan ‘buttermilk’ pancakes that can be made vegan, and you can add a scoop of vegan coconut ice cream on top for an extra $5. Unfortunately, the vegan version doesn’t have any substitutes for butter or cream, but you pay the same price. Other vegan options include Black Forest Cherry Pancakes, Blueberry Pancakes  and Lemon & Sugar Pancakes. One of the few places in Brisbane that are open 24/7.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://g.page/ThePancakeManor?share)

---

## Grazegrind

A lot of the bowls, like teriyaki bowl and hummus bowl, can be veganised by changing to falafel. They also have a good selection of smoothies.

**Vegan-Options** | [*Address & Hours*](https://g.page/grazegrind?share)
