Brisbane vegan market is held regularly in multiple locations like Mt Gravatt Showgrounds and Portside Wharf. Vegan stalls gather and create an ethical, sustainable and cruelty-free event.

Indulge yourself with delicious food like sushi, burgers, pizza, cupcake, tacos and much more! You would want to try everything here!

There is also a great selection of fashion, personal care products and crafts. All vegan and cruelty-free.

The entry is free and there are free parking available.

Multiple location. Check the links for the latest locations.
