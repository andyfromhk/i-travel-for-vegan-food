Saido in Jiyugaoka offers an outstanding fine dining experience focused entirely on innovative vegan Japanese cuisine. This reservation-only restaurant is celebrated for its creative use of vegetables and mushrooms, crafting dishes like vegan eel, katsu, noodles, and curry, alongside exquisite bento sets. The presentation is artistic and playful, adding to the fun. Diners consistently rave about the incredible flavours, meticulous attention to detail, and top-notch, intimate service where staff patiently explain each course. Vegan sake and wine are also available. While pricey, Saido is widely regarded as one of the best vegan dining experiences in Tokyo, impressing vegans and non-vegans alike. Reservations are essential (no walk-ins) and must be made via their website. Closed Wednesdays.
