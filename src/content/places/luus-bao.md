LUU's BAO is a specialty shop serving delicious vegan baos. Located next to a busy road, the setting may not be the most serene, but the great food quality more than makes up for it.

The bao I tried - Kakiage tempura with wasabi tartare sauce - was fresh and delicious with perfectly soft buns and crispy veggie tempura. The sauce is creamy, though the wasabi flavour could be stronger. The prices are reasonable for the large size of the baos. If you want something sweet afterwards, try one of their freshly fried doughnuts.
