Red Ginger has been a Byron Bay favourite since 2002. It's part Asian grocery store, part dumpling house and part gift shop. It's a beautiful store to wander through, with handmade gifts, ceramics and teaware alongside shelves of Asian groceries.

The front of the shop has a hot food cupboard with quite a few vegan options. The red bean bun and soy skin spring roll were tasty. The skin on the gyoza was a bit dry, but that's to be expected when it had been sitting in the cupboard for a while.

**Tip**: Red Ginger also sells roast duck, pork and other meat dishes, so check the labels or ask the staff which items in the hot food cupboard are vegan. There's a second store in Bangalow (38 Byron St), a 15-minute drive inland, if you're visiting the village.
