Coco Bliss hits the mark if you're looking for a healthy smoothie bowl fix in Brisbane. I really enjoy the decadent Ferrero smoothie bowl which is vegan-friendly and has rich choc fudge sauce and hazelnut crunch. All their smoothie and protein bowls are vegan-friendly, but do double-checking that your chosen granola topping is honey-free.

While they offer some hot savoury items like vegan cheese toasties and falafel bowls, they don't stand out as much as the smoothie bowls. For example, the falafel bowl is a bit on the bland side in terms of flavours. Be sure to check carefully when ordering savoury options, as Coco Bliss also serves meat. You'll find several branches across the city, including handy locations within Chermside and Carindale Westfield shopping centres.
