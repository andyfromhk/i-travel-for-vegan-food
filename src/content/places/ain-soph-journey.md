The Shinjuku branch of the popular vegan restaurant chain - Ain Soph Journey - is conveniently located between Shinjuku Station and Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden. As the place is not particularly large and is a popular among tourists, you’re likely to have to wait if you're looking to fine during lunch and dinner hours; booking ahead might be a good idea.

The food here is delicious and of high quality. They specialise in Japanese fusion dishes, though prices are on the more expensive side. If you have a sweet tooth, definitely try their signature 'heavenly' pancakes, which are soft, fluffy, and pretty "heavenly". They regularly have seasonal pancake flavours like strawberry pancakes in Spring, and chestnut ones in Autumn. Other than the pancake, they also serve some tasty, healthy savoury options, including teriyaki chick'n rice bowl, curry rice, pasta and more.
