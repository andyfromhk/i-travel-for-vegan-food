For vegan gelato lovers, Premarché Gelateria near Nakameguro station is a must-visit. While not fully vegan, they boast a huge and impressive variety of high-quality vegan gelato flavors, including creative Japanese-inspired options (like shiso or fermented cheesecake) alongside rich classics like premium chocolate. Vegan cones are available. Staff are helpful, and you can often sample flavors if you ask. The gelato is considered among the best vegan versions in Tokyo by many. Just be prepared for the premium price tag. They also offer coffee (with soy milk option) and tea. Note the pay-first, get-ticket system for ordering.
