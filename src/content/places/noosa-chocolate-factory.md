Noosa Chocolate Factory is a chocolate specialty shop located in Brisbane CBD. They offer a variety of chocolates, including some delicious vegan options. Vegan products are conveniently marked with a "vegan friendly" symbol on the packaging.

The shop has many choices such as fruits wrapped in chocolate, Turkish delight chocolate, chocolate with desiccated coconut, coffee chocolate and more. They also serve chocolate drinks. I prefer the hot chocolate drink as they are even more rich and delicious compared to the iced ones.

Prices are on the higher side. For example, a vegan milk chocolate costs about $10, including the surcharge for non-dairy milk.
