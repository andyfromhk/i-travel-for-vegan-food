A good vegan option for Brisbane's Northside near North Lakes, Vegan Thai-riffic isn't a fully vegan restaurant as you may expect, but a separate vegan menu within an omni Thai restaurant.

Expect classic Thai dishes like massaman curry, pad see ew, tom yum, and satay skewers. Overall, the flavours are enjoyable, with the pad see ew being particularly good, though the curry is too sweet for me.
