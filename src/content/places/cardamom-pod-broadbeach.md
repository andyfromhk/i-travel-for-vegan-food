Beautiful cafe in the Broadbeach area. It's a vegetarian cafe with many dishes that are vegan or veganisable.

Food is tasty and beautifully presented. Other than the a la carte menu, they do all-you-can-eat buffet daily with dishes like curry, desserts and more.
