Onari Yokocho is a Japanese omni restaurant that offers a good selection of clearly marked vegan dishes on a separate English menu. The vegan options include a tonkotsu-style ramen, sushi and Japanese curry. The prices are relatively high for the portion, but with its convenient location (just a few minutes from Kamakura station) and the clear vegan options, it's still a solid choice.
