Kem-Kon impresses with a huge variety of veganised Thai dishes on their menu. It's one of those spots you could return to again and again and always find something new to try. They have a few branches in Bangkok, including one near Khaosan Road and one relatively close to IconSiam. The branch I visited in Phahurat is in Bangkok's 'Little India', and you can see the local life of a busy intersection below as you eat if you go on the second floor.

The menu covers everything you could hope for, from creamy Khao soi to classic Tom yum and pad Thai. I was excited to finally try a vegan sai ua, the famous northern Thai sausage. Their version, made from banana blossom, was fragrant with lemongrass and lime flavours with a unique texture. My personal favourite, though, was their take on Hainanese chicken rice. The plant-based 'chicken' was tender, and the dipping sauce was packed with garlic, ginger, and soy flavours. It came with a simple, clear soup that was perfect for cleansing the palate between bites.
