If you want to satisfy your vegan sweet tooth at a super cute bakery, Pippa's Pantry should be your next destination.

The pastries and desserts taste great. You can expect to find a variety of cupcakes, croissants, savoury options and more. My personal favourite is her iced vovo cookie and crookie (cookie croissant).

She's open on Fridays and Saturdays at the time of writing. Other than this Camp Hill location, you can also find her baked goods at QLD vegan markets and various Westfield shopping centre popups.
