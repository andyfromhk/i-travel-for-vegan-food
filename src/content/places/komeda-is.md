If you're familiar with the classic Japanese "kissaten" coffee shop chains, then you probably have heard of Komeda Coffee.

This Ginza branch is their all-vegan venture called Komeda Is. With an interior featuring wood, plants, and plenty of natural lighting, it offers a modern and green twist on the traditional kissaten ambiance.

The food menu here is just as impressive, veganising a wide range of classic Japanese coffee shop dishes. You’ll find delicious options like katsu cutlet sandwich, avocado teriyaki burger, tofu fritters, and more. The katsu sandwich is a classic 'kissaten' dish, and this vegan version features vegan cutlet with a crispy outside and a firm meaty texture, and a tangy, slightly spicy sauce.

The drink selection will also impress, featuring hard-to-find treats like Vienna coffee which comes with vegan whipped cream. And at the time of writing, you get a piece of soft toast for free until 11am with any drink order.
