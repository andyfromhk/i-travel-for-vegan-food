Brought to you by the team behind the popular vegan cafe, Blendlove, this vegan bakehouse is a welcome addition to the Surfer's Paradise area. Its convenient location, just a few minutes' walk from both the Cypress Avenue tram stop and the beach, makes it a great, accessible spot for vegan foodies.

The bakery cafe impresses with a wide array of freshly baked goods, with almost everything made in-house. You can expect to find soft fragrant cookies, decadent brownies, and fresh sourdough. For the perfect flaky pastry, they have also sourced delicious croissants all the way from Sydney's vegan bakery, Oh My Days.

Among the standout items, the cookie dough croissant is an absolute must-try. It has the perfect combination of a crispy, flaky croissant with a rich cookie dough filling. The housemade cookies are another highlight with their soft and chewy texture that I love personally.
