Kuan Yin has recently undergone an ownership change, shifting its menu towards Thai-style dishes while retaining some Taiwanese dishes from the previous owner.

On their all-vegan menu, you’ll find Asian classics like fried rice, curries, hot pots and more.

The bento box I tried came with two small dishes (tofu and stir-fried veggies on the day I visited), a salad, pickles, rice, and fruit, in addition to an entree of your choice. I chose the snow fish, which tasted great with good texture, but could benefit from a bit more sauce to complement the rice.
