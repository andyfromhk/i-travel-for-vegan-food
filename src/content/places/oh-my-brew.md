Oh My Brew is a small coffee shop in South Brisbane that shares its location with the Sweet and Green bakery. This unique setup allows customers to get the matcha drinks and coffee from Oh My Brew, alongside a nice selection of fresh pastries and baked goods from Sweet and Green. They have a few outdoor seats available, making it a nice little spot to enjoy a coffee and a treat.

The main draw for foodies is the ready access to vegan pastries, and baked goods, many of which are vegan-friendly. You can find everything from delightful croissants to brioche and focaccia. But the vegan options are not marked explicitly, so make sure to ask the staff to confirm which options are vegan before ordering.

Oh My Brew specialises at drinks, with a special focus on matcha drinks. The ube matcha latte I tried was nice and strong, with an earthy and tasty profile. While some drinks like the Biscoff latte can be subtle in flavour, the staff is happy to customize your drink if you ask for a stronger flavour.
