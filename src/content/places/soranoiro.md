Conveniently located in Tokyo Station's busy Ramen Street (downstairs, before the ticket gates), Soranoiro Nippon serves meat but offers clearly marked vegan ramen options. Ordering is via an efficient ticket machine (menu mostly Japanese, but vegan options labeled in English). A vegan dandan ramen is available. However, reviews on the ramen itself are mixed; some find it a decent, quick option, while others report changes in recipe leading to a less impressive, potentially oily or overly spicy bowl with fewer toppings. Portions are generous. It's a viable spot if you need a quick vegan ramen fix right in the station, especially for mixed groups, but manage expectations regarding taste.
