Particle Cafe is a vegan cafe in Avondale Heights. Everything is vegan with many gluten free options. Expect to find classic cafe comfort food like scrambled tofu toast, BLT sandwich and schnitzel jackfruit burger. They also have a range of salad, smoothie bowls and smoothies for those who’s looking for something healthier.
