A doughnut specialty bakery with clearly marked vegan options including original, mocha and red bean. You can also find doughnuts filled with tofu whipped cream.

The doughnuts have a fluffy, bread-y texture. The mocha doughnut I tried had subtle flavours. However, the soy latte I got was pretty average. They have a few seats inside for dining in.
