On your way from Kase station to see the big buddha at Kotoku-in, you'll find Cafe Stand Blossom. This little all-vegan cafe is such a gem with delicious veganised Japanese desserts, like the chocolate ganache monaka and red bean dorayaki. You'll also find muffin, ice cream sundae and banana cake.

Don't miss their 'amazake' drinks, which is a non-alcoholic drink made from fermented rice. Its natural sweetness goes very well with matcha.
