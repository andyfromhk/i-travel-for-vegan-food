When you first walk in Little Digs, you will be greeted with the funky colourful decor.

Not only do they serve some delicious classic cafe food, they offer some innovative dishes that are hard to find elsewhere. One good example is their croffle, which is a croissant pressed into a waffle. The result is a flaky, buttery waffle that is super delicious. Other dishes like vegan macarons and Shibuya toast are also special and creative.

Their other, more classic choices, like burgers and burritos are also well-made and beautifully presented. The portions are generous here. If you are in Brisbane, I'd highly recommend visiting Little Digs.
