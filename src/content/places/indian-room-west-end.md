This Indian restaurant in West End has a good selection of clearly marked vegan dishes.

In the lunch menu, the options with VGO can be made vegan. And for dinner, they have a separate vegan menu with quite a lot of options. The vegan butter chicken rice bowl that I tried came with rice, pickled onion and pappadum. Overall it’s quite tasty with a creamy tomato-y butter chicken sauce. It has a generous amount of soy chunks. The rice is also well cooked.
