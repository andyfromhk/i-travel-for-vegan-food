Flour of Life bakery in Helensvale is a dream come true for vegans. You will find many of your childhood favourites veganised here, like cream doughnut, lamington and custard tart.

Other than sweets, they also have a good selection of savoury pies, tarts, sandwiches and more. You probably need to visit multiple times to try more of their stuff.

Keep in mind that they don't have seatings at the store.
