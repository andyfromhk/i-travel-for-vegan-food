Alaska Zwei is a cozy, fully vegan cafe with a great atmosphere and friendly staff, open from breakfast time. They offer satisfying set meals (like brown rice or curry plates), rice bowls, and filled bagels – the tofu katsu sandwich on focaccia is particularly popular and highly recommended. You can also find a selection of respectable pastries, cakes, and coffee. It's considered a favorite vegan spot in Tokyo by some reviewers due to the quality food and welcoming vibe. Keep in mind they close relatively early (6 pm), so it's not an option for dinner, and one visitor noted needing to purchase a drink.
