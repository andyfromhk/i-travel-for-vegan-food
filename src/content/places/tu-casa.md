Tu Casa is a charming Korean vegan cafe with a strong commitment to sustainability, featuring a zero-waste store on site. They serve classic delicious Korean dishes including bibimbap, gimbap, and BBQ bowl with grilled soy meat. Don't miss their baked goods; the lemon cake is a particular favourite. The vibe is cute, calm, and friendly, run by lovely owners. With limited seatings, I'd recommend arriving early if you're looking to dine in.
