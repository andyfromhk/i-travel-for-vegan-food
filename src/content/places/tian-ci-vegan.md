Located a short walk from Redfern station, Tian Ci Vegan serves an impressive range of affordable, vegan Chinese dishes. They create traditional Chinese dishes with vegan meat, making it a go-to for those craving classics like Mongolian 'beef' or sweet and sour 'pork'. Popular recommendations also include the crispy deep-fried mushrooms and the authentic-tasting 'chicken'. While the setting isn't overly fancy, the service is notably friendly and good value. The restaurant has a large space and courtyard seatings, though it's worth noting they may sometimes focus primarily on takeaway which means a bit of a wait for food.
