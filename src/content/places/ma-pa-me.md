Ma Pa Me is a great vegan-option restaurant located in Southbank, Brisbane that serves delicious Southeast Asian cuisine. The restaurant has a separate vegan menu that offers some great options.

One of my favourites here include the pumpkin white curry and the vegan 'ma' gado gado, both of which are rich and flavourful. For the gado gado, I'd recommend asking them to make it with more sauce if possible. It is perfect by itself or with a bowl of rice. Another stand out dish is the jackfruit rendang which has a strong, umami flavour.

Some of the dishes I've tried here are nice but too oily, including the nasi goreng and pad Thai.
