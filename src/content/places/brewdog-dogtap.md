BrewDog has two venues in Brisbane: DogTap, its brewery in Murarrie, and a bar in Fortitude Valley. I've been to both, and on my last visit I went to DogTap, which is very spacious. Both have great vegan options, including a few pizzas, a parmy, cauliflower wings, burgers and even vegan milkshakes.

I went for their 2-for-1 pizza deal and tried the mushroom pizza and the eggplant parmy pizza, which worked out to about $13–14 each. They're decent woodfired pizzas. The base is slightly dense, but they were better than I expected from a brewery, and I'm happy they use vegan cheese. I also really loved the vegan milkshake; I got the Oreo flavour.
