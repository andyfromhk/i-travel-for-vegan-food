Specialising in fresh, hearty takeaway salads, Botanica has several Brisbane locations. While the salads are not marked, the staff are knowledgeable and can guide you in choosing the vegan ones. Often most salads on offer are vegan.

The salads are delicious and nourishing. I particular like the potato & pea, freekeh & eggplant, or the Cypriot grain salad. The portion is also pretty generous. Keep an eye out for their occasional vegan chocolate brownie too.
