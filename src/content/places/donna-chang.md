Donna Chang is an upscale Chinese restaurant housed in an impressive old bank building in Brisbane, with high ceilings and elegant decor. For vegans, the main menu have a decent amount of vegan dishes to pick from, including options like dan dan noodles and dumplings. They also have a vegan banquet (about $130 per person) if you want to try a bit of everything.
