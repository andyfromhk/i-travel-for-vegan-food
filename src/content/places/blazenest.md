*Permanently closed since November 2023*

Overall I enjoyed the meal here. Everything is tasty and well made, and the service is friendly as well. The food is also good value with decent portion.

One thing to keep in mind is that almost every item on the menu is fried/deep-fried. Although the dishes are made well (crispy & crunchy without too oily), it’s possible to feel a bit heavy afterwards with all the fried food.

But if you like fried food with an Asian flair, you’ll love this restaurant!

They have only 3-4 tables in the restaurant so definitely call ahead to book if you are planning to go!
