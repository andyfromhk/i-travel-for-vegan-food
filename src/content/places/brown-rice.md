Tucked away near Omotesando, Brown Rice is an upscale, fully vegan restaurant that offers a serene courtyard setting for enjoying their traditional Japanese lunch sets.

Brown Rice focuses mainly on healthy, beautifully presented Japanese cuisine using macrobiotic principles. The menu features a small selection of delicious seasonal set meals that change regularly. If it's your first time here, try the lunch set with bamboo basket with steamed vegetables. Staff are attentive and helpful, and an English menu is available.

It’s a great spot for a slow, aesthetically pleasing meal, but keep in mind it's quite pricey and the menu is rather small.
