This poke bowl chain offers a couple of solid vegan options built around either tofu or mushrooms, both packed with a variety of healthy, whole-food ingredients. While primarily focused on fish, they also have tacos that can be made vegan by requesting no mayo and by choosing tofu or mushroom as the protein. Be aware that due to the fish-heavy menu, the smell can be quite strong if you sit inside the restaurant.
