Bad Hombres is a popular vegan and gluten-free serving Mexican cuisine. The menu focuses on shareable plates perfect for groups. Standout recommendations often include the flavourful, generously portioned cauliflower dish and the Birria mushroom tacos. Other popular choices include their 'fish' tacos and the unique miso eggplant tacos. For something sweet, go for the creme brulee for dessert. They also offer creative cocktails. While the food is good, some may find the flavour less bright than traditional Mexican fare, and the prices could be on the expensive side.
