Tin Tin Vegetarian is a popular takeaway chain that’s perfect for a quick bite or an emergency snack.

The menu is filled with classic Hong Kong vegetarian food, like bean curd rolls, radish cake, sticky rice rolls, and various wheat gluten pieces. If you're in the mood for something more filling, you can also opt for stir-fry noodles or their daily rice set.

My personal favourite here is the steam rice noodle rolls, also known as cheung fun (腸粉). This traditional Hong Kong street snack has a delicious blend of sweet and salty flavours from soy sauce, a sweet red sauce, sesame peanut sauce, and topped with roasted sesame. For those who like a kick, drizzle some extra chilli sauce on top.
