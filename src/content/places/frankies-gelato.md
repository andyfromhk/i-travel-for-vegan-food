A short stroll from Main Beach, Frankies is a Sicilian-inspired gelateria that churns all its gelato on site with local ingredients and no added colours or preservatives. Around 40% of the flavours are vegan, so there's always a good selection to choose from.

The gelato here is creamy and delicious. I tried the matcha macadamia flavour, which had a great balance of nutty macadamia and strong matcha.

**Tip**: Vegan flavours are marked "VGN" on the menu. You can also have your gelato in a Sicilian-style brioche bun, but brioche usually contains egg and butter, so check with the staff before ordering it. Frankies stays open late and is just a few doors from No Bones, so it's an easy second dessert stop after dinner.
