Just 100m from Main Beach, No Bones is Byron Bay's go-to spot for an all-vegan dinner with cocktails. The food is good quality overall, with a slightly fancy feel and smaller portions, so it's a great pick for a date night or a special occasion.

In my opinion, the sweet side of the menu is even stronger than the savoury. The tiramisu is the standout, and I think it's one of the best tiramisus I've ever had. Don't leave without ordering it.

On the savoury side, the carbonara has a good flavour overall, though I was expecting the 'vegan egg yolk' on top to be runny. The sticky tofu comes with a sauce that was a bit too bitter for my taste. The service, however, was amazing from start to finish.

**Tip**: No Bones runs $20 Carbonara Mondays (all night, currently scheduled until 30 November 2026) and live jazz on the first Friday of every month. It's dinner only, so book ahead for weekends.
