Close to the Teramachi-Dori shopping street, Pelgag is a small restaurant with a veg menu with vegan options marked. They offer rotating vegan options like curry rice, fried rice and sautéed vegetable dishes. As there are only around 10 seats, and the cafe is often run by just one person, there could be a wait for food.
