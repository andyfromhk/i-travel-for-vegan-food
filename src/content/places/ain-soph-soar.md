Tucked away near Ikebukuro's Sunshine City, AIN SOPH.Soar provides a tranquil, fully vegan dining experience. This branch offers the brand's signature plant-based Japanese fusion meals, seasonal desserts, and friendly service in a cool, calm setting. Menu items like burgers and green curry have been enjoyed by visitors. While the desserts are tempting, be aware they, and the menu overall, can lean towards the expensive side. They offer a kids menu, making it family-friendly. It's a lovely escape from the city bustle, but it's wise to confirm their current opening hours before heading over, and note they are closed on Tuesdays.
