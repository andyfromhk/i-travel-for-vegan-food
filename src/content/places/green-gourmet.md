Green Gourmet Vegan is a long-standing, fully vegan favourite in St Leonards, offering a wide array of Asian cuisine. This popular spot features everything from yum cha and noodle dishes to stir-fries and an impressive selection of vegan cakes and desserts. Recommendations include their 'prawn' dumplings and carrot cake during yum cha, alongside flavourful mains like Sichuan cauliflower and laksa. The food is overall well-made, but some may find the yum cha variety limited. Previously, they did pay-by-weight lunch buffet but have stopped doing that recently.
