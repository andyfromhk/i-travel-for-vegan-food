Situated within Yokokawa Park, Sasaya Cafe offers a mostly vegan menu (honey products are sold in the shop, so double-check ingredients if concerned) in a relaxed setting. They serve tasty dishes featuring organic vegetables, like tempeh cutlets, curry, sandwiches, and daily specials, alongside drinks. It's known for being affordable and having friendly staff. However, be aware that meal portions are often described as small, more like snack-sized. The park location means it can sometimes be noisy with children, but it also offers a dog-friendly terrace. It's a pleasant, budget-friendly spot for a light bite or coffee break.
