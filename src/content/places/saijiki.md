(TW VEGAN NIGHT MARKET is previously known as Saijiki)

Saijiki is a vegan Taiwanese restaurant offering authentic cuisine at great prices. As a restaurant that doesn’t use the "five pungent roots" like onions and garlic, their food is still tasty and flavourful. You can find classic Taiwanese dishes like mapo tofu, deep-fried "big chicken" cutlet, bamboo basket-steamed dumplings, and more.

For a satisfying and budget-friendly meal, come for lunch and get one of their lunch sets. The Taiwanese mince rice set comes with dumplings, veggies and soup in addition to the main. They use a ticket machine ordering process - simply purchase your meal ticket from the vending machine near the entrance, then hand it over to the friendly staff who will promptly prepare your food.
