Patsy's is a Mediterranean-inspired wine bar and restaurant. The menu is vegetarian but the vegan options are not clearly marked on the menu, so you'd need to check with the staff for details. The focus is on wine, and all the wines are vegan-friendly. The prices are on the higher side.
