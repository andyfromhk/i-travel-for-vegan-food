An all-vegan restaurant in Paddington, Brisbane. The atmosphere here is relaxing, and staff is helpful and friendly.

The food is general good with choices like burgers, mac & cheese, pancakes, etc. However, their signature CFC, which is a fried chicken dish with mashed potatoes, fries, slaw and gravy, was disappointing with small chicken pieces and huge portion of potatoes.
