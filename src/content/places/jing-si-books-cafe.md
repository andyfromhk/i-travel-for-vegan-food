Jing Si Books & Cafe is a nice hidden gem in Brisbane. Located in the quiet streets in Salisbury, this all-vegan cafe offers a calming atmosphere that's great for a relaxing meal.

The menu is on the simpler, lighter side. I tried the Jing Si rice bowl set, which includes a comforting miso soup and a colourful rice bowl topped with a vegan egg made from tofu and pumpkin, spongy tofu, avocado, vegan meat floss, fresh salad and more. They also have a great selection of teas and lattes, including unique ones like genmaicha and black rice latte.
