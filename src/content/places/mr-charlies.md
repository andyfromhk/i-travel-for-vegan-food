Mr. Charlie's serves up an entirely vegan menu designed to replicate the classic McDonald's experience. If you're missing Macca's flavours, this is your spot. Expect familiar favourites like burgers that closely mimic the taste and sauciness of the originals, alongside crispy fries and vegan nuggets. The flavours are pretty close to the non-vegan counterpart, making it pretty nostalgia. The vegan "Big Mac" is a popular choice here. Just be prepared for prices that are higher than typical fast food. Also note that side sauces come in small portions and cost extra.
