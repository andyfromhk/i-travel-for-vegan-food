Located on a quiet street in West End, Grown is a modern vegan cafe with an emphasis on fresh local produce and seasonal menu.

Food is well presented and delicious. I love the creative details of the dishes. For example, the avo toast comes with a cheese made of cashew and sesame. Or the way they create a crispy, creme brûlée-like crust of the French toast by torching it with sugar.

For brunch, they offer a more cafe style menu. While in the evening, they offer a more fine-dining style.
