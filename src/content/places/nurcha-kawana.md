Nurcha is a great place to visit for vegans. You can find delicious food, groceries, crystals, decor, and so much more here.

The selection for their grocery and homeware is pretty impressive. From vegan snacks to big home fixtures, you will spend some time going through all the items. The prices are also reasonable once you joined the free membership.

The Nurcha cafe also has a wide range of choices, from healthy acai bowls to freshly made pizzas. The food I've tried are well made and delicious. There is also plenty of seating, both indoor and outdoor, where you can sit and relax for an afternoon.
