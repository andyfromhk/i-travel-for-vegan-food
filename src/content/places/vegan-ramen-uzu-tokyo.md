Vegan Ramen UZU Tokyo offers a unique dining experience, located exclusively inside the TeamLab Planets digital art museum – meaning you must have a museum ticket to access it. They serve fully vegan ramen, specialty teas, muffins, and ice cream (flavours like matcha, yuzu, strawberry). It's a convenient option for a meal or snack during your museum visit, with outdoor seating available. However, reviews are strongly mixed. Some visitors rave about the delicious ramen and ice cream, calling it one of the best things they've eaten. Others find the ramen basic and lacking toppings, criticize the ice cream quality, and express frustration about the restricted access and potential wait times. If eating before your exhibit, book your museum ticket for at least an hour earlier.
