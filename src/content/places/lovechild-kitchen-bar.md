Lovechild is a vegan restaurant + bar in the Mermaid Beach area.

They serve some classic fast food like burgers and fried chicken. The quality is quite good, I especially enjoy the fried chicken tender for its texture. However, some may find that the price is a bit high for the portion you get.

One side of the shop is dedicated to their fashion and accessories - Lovechild Boutique + Beauty.
