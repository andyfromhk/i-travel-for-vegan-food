Jumi's Cafe is a 100% vegan cafe, with a focus on healthy options like acai bowls, smoothies, wraps, salads, and loaded toasties. They also offer some vegan pantry staples and deli items. It's a good spot for a takeaway or a casual, quick meal, with limited seating.
