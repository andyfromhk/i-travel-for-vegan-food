Lord of the Fries is a fast-food chain that is entirely vegan. They offer the classic veganised fast food including burgers, hot dogs, nuggets, and their signature fries with a huge array of different sauces. Try the 'Biggie' burger which is their version of a Big Mac, and get a cookies and cream milkshake to complete your meal.
