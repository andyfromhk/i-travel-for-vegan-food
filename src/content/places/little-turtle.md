Little Turtle is a popular all-vegan Thai eatery in Enmore, loved for its health-conscious cooking and chic industrial-chic atmosphere. Rather than using faux meats, the kitchen focuses on using tofu and vegetables to deliver authentic Thai flavours in beautifully presented dishes.

Standouts often include the Pad See Ew and green curry fried rice, though some diners note the curries can lean slightly sweeter. Little Turtle has multiple dining areas, including a charming outdoor terrace decorated with fairy lights. It's also a BYO restaurant. Expect friendly, efficient service, but be mindful that parking nearby can be challenging as it's located on a main road.
