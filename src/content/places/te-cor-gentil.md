Te Cor Gentil is a highly recommended fully vegan bakery offering an amazing and extensive selection of both sweet and savory baked goods. You can find everything from croissants, various bread loaves, and pizza slices to Japanese favourites like melon pan and anpan, alongside donuts, cream choux, and sandwiches. Everything is praised for being delicious and great value. It's a small spot with limited outdoor seating (locals suggest enjoying purchases in the nearby park), so plan for takeaway. Be sure to arrive early, as popular items sell out quickly. Check their Instagram for irregular closing days.
