Located in the upscale Ginza district, AIN SOPH.Ginza offers a fine dining, fully vegan experience across multiple floors. The ground floor features a patisserie and juice/smoothie cafe, while the upper floors house the restaurant serving beautifully presented Japanese fusion lunches, tea-time desserts, and dinners. Reservations are strongly recommended, often needed well in advance, and might be required to access the full menu, including options like elaborate bento boxes or pre-ordered sushi. While the food (including their famous pancakes) and service are excellent, be prepared for a significantly higher price point compared to other vegan spots or even other Ain Soph locations. It's best for a special occasion planned ahead.
