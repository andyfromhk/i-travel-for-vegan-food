Located in the heart of Kobe near Chinatown and Sannomiya station, Veggie Junkies Burger is an all-vegan, takeaway-only spot. This place is run by a cute couple and offers a limited setting area for customers to wait for their orders, but there’s no dine-in option. I’d recommend going to a nearby park, or to the seaside area where Kobe Port Tower is to enjoy the burger.

After going upstairs, you can order through a ticket machine, hand the ticket to the staff, and they will prepare your burger. They offer a small menu of 3 burgers: teriyaki mushroom, curry bean and tomato, and desserts like soy milk pudding or agar agar jelly.
