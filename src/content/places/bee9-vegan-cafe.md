Bee9 Vegan Cafe is a relatively unknown gem in Osaka serving up delicious vegan food. This cozy cafe has a cozy, inviting ambiance and is conveniently located near the famous Tenmangu Shrine. Though the menu is concise, it offers excellent main dishes like buddha bowls, bibimbap, and tantan ramen.

I tried and loved the kimbap/gimbap plate, featuring colourful kimbap stuffed with hearty veggies and flavourful fried soy meat. For dessert, you'll find hard-to-find items in Japan like acai parfait, alongside pound cakes and cupcakes.

The cafe is relaxing and peaceful, and the staff is friendly. English menu is available.
