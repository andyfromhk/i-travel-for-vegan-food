Serving Southeast Asian flavours, P'Nut Asian Kitchen is a restaurant chain that provides vegan options. Some of the stir-fries like satay or teriyaki can be made vegan by choosing the vegetable base. You can also consider adding extra tofu in the stir-fry. Other vegan-friendly options include Singapore noodles (pick the vegetable base) and vegetable spring rolls.
