Conveniently located in the city, Govinda's offers hearty vegetarian and vegan dishes. The staff is knowledgeable and can point out which dishes are vegan and which ones are not.

Portions here are generous. You can choose from various 'feasts', ranging from a simple rice and curry combo of the 'small feast', to a large plate with kofta, chutney, and salad when you order the 'large feast'. You can also request some free poppadums. While the taste won't wow you, Govinda's is still a reliable choice for a satisfying and filling meal.
