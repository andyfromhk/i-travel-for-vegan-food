Fishbowl is well-known for its fresh, vibrant, and healthy bowls, and they offer some solid options for vegans by using Fable's tasty shiitake mushroom meat.

In the menu, the mushroom street food box and Mush Love bowl are vegan by default and they use Fable's mushroom meat. The mushroom box is a great budget option with mushroom meat, sesame dressing, umami cashew crunch, house slaw, and a base of sushi rice. And the Mush Love Bowl is a larger meal featuring Fable meat alongside a variety of vegetables tossed in a lemon olive oil and soy sauce dressing, topped with crunchy wasabi peas.

For anyone wanting an even heartier meal, you also have the option to add an extra serving of the Fable mushroom meat to any bowl.
