Yellow is an upscale, fully vegan restaurant and bar in Potts Point, highly regarded for its exceptional dining experience. It currently offers a seasonal 6-course tasting menu, known for creative and beautifully presented dishes that impress even non-vegans with their rich flavours and textures. Optional wine pairing is available. The atmosphere is sophisticated which makes it perfect for a special occasion. Given its popularity, booking ahead is recommended.
