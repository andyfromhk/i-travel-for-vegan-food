Located in the bustling Akihabara area, Kyushu Jangara Ramen is a traditional ramen shop that serves meat but offers a vegan ramen option. While they previously had two choices, recent reports (as of March 2025, needs confirmation) suggest only one vegan ramen bowl is currently available, featuring vegetables and tofu (you can add vegan char siu). Despite the limited choice, reviewers find the vegan ramen delicious and authentic. The atmosphere is trendy but seating is very limited, leading to likely queues and a quick eat-and-go experience. It's a worthwhile option if you need vegan ramen in Akihabara, but manage expectations regarding choice and wait times.
