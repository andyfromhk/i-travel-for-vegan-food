Miss Sina is a popular, fully vegan bakery and restaurant that has been delighting Marrickville locals and visitors since September 2022.Tucked away in a quiet spot, it's known for its incredibly creative menu and warm, friendly service.

They offer an all-day brunch menu featuring innovative dishes like miso butter cabbage and corn agnolotti, alongside sandwiches and breakfast platters. However, the baked goods are true highlights, with German-inspired pastries, cakes, fluffy donuts, famous cinnamon scrolls, and savoury options like vegan sausage rolls often stealing the show. It's a busy spot, especially for baked goods like crookies and kunefe which can sell out, so arriving early or booking for meals is recommended. Keep an eye on their social media for specials.
