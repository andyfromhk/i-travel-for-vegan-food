If you’re like me, you probably have hoped for a vegan friendly sushi train restaurant before. Luckily, Midori By Tao's may just be what you’ve been looking for. This vegetarian sushi train restaurant where most of the offerings are vegan, and the items containing egg or dairy are clearly marked. They offer a wide variety of sushi rolls as well as a separate hot food menu with dishes like laksa and ramen, which can be ordered via QR code and delivered by a little electric train. They also offer a more upscale vegan Kaiseki (multi-course Japanese tasting menu) experience upstairs.
