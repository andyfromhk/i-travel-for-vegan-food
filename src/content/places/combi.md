Combi is one of Byron Bay's best-known brunch cafes, with an organic menu of açaí bowls, smoothies, toasts and specialty coffee. There are good vegan options across the menu, and it opens at 7am every day, so it's an easy breakfast stop before the beach.

I tried my friend's loaded banana bread, which was a highlight. It was toasted with crispy edges while still soft in the middle, and loaded with vegan cream and fruit. The mocha I had, however, was a bit watery.
