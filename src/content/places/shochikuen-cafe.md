Shochiku-en is a cute, fully vegan cafe conveniently located right by Kappabashi street. It offers a nice selection of treats and light meals, including coffee with plant milk options, cakes (like their signature rainbow cake), muffins, nut tarts, steamed buns, small burgers, and pizza, with some gluten-free options available. While space is limited, it's a good spot for a coffee break or a light bite. Reviews highlight the delicious baked goods (mousse cake, rose cookies, muffins, tarts) and good coffee, though one diner found a burger slightly oily. It's a worthwhile stop for vegans exploring the Kappabashi area.
