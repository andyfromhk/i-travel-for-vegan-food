Wrapture is a cafe in West End, Brisbane specialising on wraps. They have 4 wraps that can be veganised: Falafel, sultan, satay tofu and zen tofu. They also usually have a selection of vegan sweets and desserts next to the counter.

Keep in mind that you'd need to specify a vegan wrap when you order as the default pita wrap is not vegan. The vegan wrap is AU$1 extra.

The wraps are freshly made and delicious. And the filling is generous.
