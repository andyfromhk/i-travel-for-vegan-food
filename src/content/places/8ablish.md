8ablish is a high-end vegan restaurant located near Azabudai Hills. The food here is beautifully presented and delicious, despite the relatively high price. Both dishes I tried are both solid - The baked soy bolognese penne gratin has a creamy sauce with a variety of colourful veggies and pasta. It's tasty but slightly under-seasoned. On the other hand, the Gateau au chocolate is beautifully shaped like a cacao bean, with strong decadent chocolate flavours.

While indoor seating is limited, there are plenty of outdoor seating available with a quiet, pleasant ambiance. They also have a selection of baked goods that you can buy and take home.
