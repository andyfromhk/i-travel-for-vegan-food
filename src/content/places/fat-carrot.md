Fat Carrot is a Vietnamese cafe located in the heart of West End on Boundary Street, offering a great selection for vegans with its separate vegan menu. You'll find classics like pho and rice paper rolls, but their vegan banh mi range is my favourite here, featuring options like satay chicken, lemongrass beef, and fish cake. These are typically packed with shredded carrots and other veggies, with vegan mayo. It's a good idea to specify "vegan" when ordering your banh mi, as not all options are vegan by default. The cafe offers both indoor and outdoor seating.
