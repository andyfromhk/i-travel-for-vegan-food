If you're looking for a hidden gem in Redcliffe with great vegan options, Thai Redcliffe is what you're looking for. This Thai restaurant has a surprisingly big vegan menu, including classic dishes like curry, pad Thai, and spring rolls.

During my 2 visits, the food has been consistently great. Some of the standout dishes include the Vegan Thai Redcliffe curry (creamy and flavourful, could be too spicy for some) and pineapple fried rice with BBQ pork. For the choice of mock meat, I'd recommend going with the crispy chicken.

One thing to keep in mind is that the restaurant is quite small, with only about five tables. If you're planning to visit, it's a good idea to book ahead, especially since there aren't many vegan options in the area.
