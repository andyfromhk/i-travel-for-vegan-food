Modernark Pharm is a charming cafe with a vintage vibe that offers great vegan options. All the dishes are either vegan by default or can be made vegan. You can find meals like the vegetarian plate or the curry plate, both of which may need to customise for vegans. Keep in mind that this cafe only accepts cash or Paypay.
