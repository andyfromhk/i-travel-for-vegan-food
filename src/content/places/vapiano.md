An Italian restaurant chain with locations across Brisbane (including the CBD, Garden City, and Carindale), Vapiano offers clearly marked vegan options, though expect to pay extra for substitutions like vegan cheese or vegan cream.

The Margherita pizza has a thin, crispy crust but for me it's a bit dry and the vegan cheese is not melted. The Fusilli crema di funghi has a generous amount of mushrooms, but the vegan cream version may lack the expected creaminess. A decent option if nearby, but manage expectations for the price point.
