The Wickham, a vibrant LGBTQ+ friendly venue, surprised me with its great vegan food offerings. They have vegan-friendly stone-baked pizza, burger, schnitzel, garlic bread and more. I tried their Tartufo pizza, which features truffle paste, mushrooms, vegan cheese, and topped with crispy fried enoki mushrooms. The base is well-made and chewy, and at the weekday lunch special price of $15 (usually $29), it was an absolute steal – just remember to specify 'vegan' when ordering.
