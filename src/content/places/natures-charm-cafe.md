You've probably seen Nature's Charm products in supermarkets, but I was so excited to find out they have their own vegan cafe right in Bangkok. My visit was even better than I had imagined. It's a beautiful, relaxing two-level space decorated with lots of plants. The menu is huge and incredibly creative, offering everything from Western classics like an all-day breakfast to veganised traditional Thai dishes and even Korean favourites like bibimbap and jjajangmyeon.

I tried to sample a bit of everything, and the food was just next level. Their signature fish & chips is made with banana blossom for a flaky texture, and it came with a minty mashed pea, tartar sauce and chips. As a huge fan of Taiwanese food, I found their Lu Rou Fan (braised pork rice) to be highly authentic. The mix of soy protein, mushrooms, and tofu gives a perfect texture, while the sauce has a great depth of flavours. Another highlight is the Son-in-law eggs, which is a traditional Thai dish with a sweet and tangy sauce, now made vegan with vegan boiled eggs.

Make sure to check out their shop section which carries their coconut condensed milk, cooking sauces, and even some rare products that are rarely seen outside of Thailand, like matcha spread and sugar-free condensed milk. For dessert, the matcha crepe cake was an absolute dream, with layers of whipped cream and a rich chocolate drizzle. They have other hard-to-find desserts like macarons and creme brulee. For drinks, the Vietnamese iced coffee uses their own delicious coconut condensed milk.
