Ko-So Cafe is a cute, fully vegan health food spot near Ebisu station with a cozy, albeit narrow, interior. The menu includes curries, pastas, risottos, salads, and standout dishes like flavourful pancakes and juicy, well-seasoned soy meat preparations. Desserts like tiramisu cake are also available. Staff are friendly and attentive. While the food is praised for being fresh and tasty, some diners find the portions a bit small for the price point, which can be slightly high. It's a lovely place for a healthy meal or coffee, especially if you're in the Ebisu area. Note: they are closed on Wednesdays.
