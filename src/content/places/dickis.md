Dicki’s is a 100% vegan cafe located in New Farm. The vibe is relaxing and the food is consistently high quality.

On the menu, you'll find comfort food options like the kimchi schnitzel burger, 'build-your-own-bao' plate and the breakfast burrito. I especially love their breakfast burrito which is loaded with scrambled tofu, vegan sausage, hash brown and more. They also have a specials menu which includes seasonal fluffy waffle and other dishes like mushroom arancini and satay duck wrap when I visited.
