Tian Ran is a vegan restaurant on the Gold Coast that offers classic Asian dishes. The standout dish is the crispy teriyaki chicken, which has a crispy batter and a delicious sweet sauce. But some dishes are a bit too dry, such as the satay sticks and the salt & pepper tofu.

The service is friendly and overall it's a good choice if you are looking for food on the Gold Coast
