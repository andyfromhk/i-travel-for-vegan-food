This vegan cafe located at South Port, Gold Coast, serves up some satisfying and beautiful dishes. All their dishes are so colourful and beautifully presented (taste great as well), including the coconut pancakes that comes with a date caramel sauce and coconut ice cream, and the healthier smoothie bowl which is just so beautifully put together.

But one thing that stood out the most to me is their house-made rice paper bacon which is smoky, chewy, and so flavourful. It’s used for dishes like the BBQ Cheezeburger and the Happy Hashies.

Other than these dishes, their cabinet is filled with made-in-house baked goods and meals that I’ll have to go back and try. Also, they make stunning latte art, and the owners and staff are all super friendly.
