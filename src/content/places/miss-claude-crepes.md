Miss Claude offers both sweet and savoury crepes in their separate vegan menu. Sweet crepes include classics like strawberry jam, raspberry coulis, or simple lemon & sugar, with vegan coconut ice cream available. For a savoury option, try the 'Vegatouille' featuring a hearty vegetable casserole, but you'll have to add extra if you want vegan cheese with it. While the quality is decent, the crepes can feel a bit pricey for the portion size.
