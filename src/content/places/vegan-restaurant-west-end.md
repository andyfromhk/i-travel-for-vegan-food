This vegan Asian restaurant in West End, Brisbane has a big menu. You will find dishes including Vietnamese, Thai, Chinese and more.

Honey chicken, fried rice and rosemary chicken are my go-to's. Food quality is nice but nothing too memorable. Overall value is good with generous portion.
