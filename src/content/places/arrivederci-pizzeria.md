This Milton pizzeria caters to vegans as well as meat eaters. They have dedicated and extensive sections of vegan options on their menu.

Pizzas here are rectangular-shaped, and are available in 25cm, 50cm, or impressive metre-long sizes. The vegan pizzas come with vegan cheese without extra charge. Try the Mediterranean (sun-dried tomatoes, capers, olives) or the Funghi (mixed mushrooms, truffle oil, optional vegan bacon). The vegan carbonara is creamy and generously portioned, but the flavour is mild with a lack of vegan bacon in the pasta. If you are looking for something sweet after, go for their vegan sundae with gelato options including chocolate and hazelnut.
