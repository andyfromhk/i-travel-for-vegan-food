Located on Melbourne Street in South Brisbane, Taro's Ramen has a small vegan menu with some classic Japanese dishes, including ramen, gyoza and curry rice.

The 3 different ramen choices: vegan classic, vegan red and vegan fire, are the same with different spicy levels. They taste good with a flavourful broth and chewy noodles. The toppings could be a bit more generous, as you only get 2 small pieces of tofu and veggies for the price.

The cold tomato noodles are another good choice. It's flavourful and refreshing. However, I wouldn't recommend the curry rice as it lacks toppings and not worth the price.
