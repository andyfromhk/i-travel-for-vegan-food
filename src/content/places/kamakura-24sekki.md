24sekki is a small, cozy, all-vegan bakery and cafe which is a 10 minute bus away from Kamakura station. The quiet, hideout-like atmosphere makes it a great spot for a relaxing meal. They offer simple lunch sets, with options like the vegan meat sandwich and other house-made bread. Cinnamon rolls are another popular choice if you are looking for something sweet. Keep in mind that the hours are irregular and limited, so be sure to check their website or Instagram before visiting.
