Falafel Brothers is a fully vegan chain in Tokyo with locations in Shibuya, Roppongi and Otemachi. Great option if you are looking for a hearty falafel pockets and other comfort food without breaking the bank.

Their falafel bowls and sandwiches are customisable. You can choose from 5 different ‘styles’, for example, an Israeli style with hummus and tahini sauce, or the European variation with beets, sauerkraut, and tahini sauce.

Beyond falafel, they serve a variety of international dishes like gyoza, pizza and desserts like ice cream cookie served in a mini skillet. The portion is generous at reasonable prices.
