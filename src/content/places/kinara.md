Kinara is an Indian restaurant in South Brisbane, right across from Musgrave Park, with a dedicated vegan section on the menu. The curries here are very good quality, and I especially love the pumpkin and the spinach curries. The only shame is that none of the naan is vegan, so pair your curry with rice instead.

If you're nearby, its sister restaurant Bagicha on Melbourne Street also has a vegan section, with a British-Indian 'curry house' twist.
