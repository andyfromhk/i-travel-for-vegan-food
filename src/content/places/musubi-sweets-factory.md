Musubi Sweets Factory is a fully vegan and gluten-free cake shop located clear to the Hankyu Arashiyama station. The cafe is relaxing and cozy, offering a range of cakes, tarts and even ice cream cookie sandwich. For a classic Japanese dessert, I'd recommend the matcha tofu cake which has a soft, creamy texture. However, the coffee I had was not great as it was very weak.
