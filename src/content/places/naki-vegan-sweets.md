Naki Vegan Sweets is a small, fully vegan bakery located in the Namba/Dotonbori area of Osaka. They specialise in beautifully made cakes, tarts, and cookies, with a rotating selection. The products are high quality and visually stunning. The shop itself is quite small and more of a grab-and-go spot, though there are some wall-facing seats.
