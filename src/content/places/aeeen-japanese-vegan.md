Aeeen is a Michelin-recommended Japanese restaurant tucked away in a traditional wooden house that genuinely feels like you've stepped into rural Japan. The whole place has a very calm, homestyle vibe, and I loved seeing the shelves lined with jars of fermented ingredients they use in their cooking. You can even buy some of their sauces and dressings to take home with you.

The menu is based on the Japanese Shojin Ryori style, which is traditional vegetarian cuisine eaten by monks. On the menu, you can find curry rice, rice bowls and more. Almost everything is vegan, but they do offer an egg as an add-on for some dishes.

Come with a flexible mindset as some items are limited and might sell out quickly. Two of my original picks were sold out when I arrived at around 1pm. My personal favourite was the tempe katsu curry rice. The tempeh cutlet was perfectly crispy, and the flavourful curry sauce had a little kick of spice. I also tried the tempe burg-don, which was a rice bowl with a tempeh patty covered in a tangy, gravy-like demi-glace sauce. The tofu karaage was light and spongy, though maybe a little small for the price.

The dishes show the care and passion that goes into the food. Aeeen is a recommended visit for anyone who appreciates thoughtful Japanese cuisine and wants a peaceful escape from the city.
