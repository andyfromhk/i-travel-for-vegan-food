Tucked away in the back streets near JR Nara station, Onwa is an all-vegan cafe serving incredibly photogenic and delicious plates, The highlight here is the "Vegan Delight" plate which features all the small dishes they make here, including soy protein taco, tofu patty, fried soy meat, pickles, radish cake and more. As the dishes are freshly made, be prepared for a bit of a wait.

Near the entrance, you'll also find a cabinet full of vegan treats and desserts like blueberry tart and vegan cheesecake. The staff here is friendly and English menu is available.
