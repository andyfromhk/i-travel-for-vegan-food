Pizza Madre in Marrickville is a popular, easygoing spot specialising in vegetarian, Napoli-style sourdough pizza with a tasty pizza crust. Run by the team behind Two Chaps cafe, they offer an authentic wood-fired pizza experience. The menu changes periodically (often monthly), with almost all pizzas can be made vegan using their house-made, melty vegan mozzarella. All their natural wines are vegan too. It's a small, bustling restaurant. Be aware that outside seating can be noisy due to traffic.
