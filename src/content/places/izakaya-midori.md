Izakaya Midori has a mission: to provide 100% vegan Japanese cuisine that is good for the environment, animals and your health. They envision a future where karaage doesn't need to come from chicken.

The menu is filled with Japanese classics like sushi, ramen, katsu rice bowl, etc. The dishes are as beautiful as they are delicious.

Other than the amazing food, the service here is warm and friendly. They try their best to make sure your experience is as perfect as possible.
