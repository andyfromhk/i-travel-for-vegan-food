Veganyumm is an all-vegan bakery in Everton Park with amazing baked goods. You will find a wide selection of sweet and savoury baked goods. One of their famous creations is the colourful galaxy brownie - blue, yellow, pink icing on top of a chocolate brownie. It's super rich and delicious. If you don't feel like a brownie, they also have tons of other options including the amazing cupcakes.

They also have drinks: Shakes (the Snickers one is great) and coffee.

For those who don't feel like sweet that day, they also have delicious savoury options like sausage roll, shepherd pie and more.
