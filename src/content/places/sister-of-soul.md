Sister of Soul is a vegetarian restaurant located in St Kilda, offering menus with mostly vegan options. You can expect to find a mix of international flavours, with items like okonomiyaki, nasi goreng, burgers, and various bowls. They also have a separate breakfast menu with more western dishes. With the relaxing atmosphere and friendly staff, it’s a great destination for anyone visiting St Kilda.
