*(Greenhouse Canteen on the Gold Coast is permanently closed since Sep 2023)*

Greenhouse Canteen is a vegan, gluten-free restaurant located on the Gold Coast. Overall the food and service are good, but not particularly memorable.

The standout dish is the Korean rice cake which has a flavourful Korean sweet chilli jam and the rice cakes are deliciously crispy. But the truffle mac and cheese is a bit of a let down with an underwhelming truffle taste and overcooked macaroni. The pumpkin Alfredo gnocchi is good but not as tasty as the one served at the now-closed South Brisbane branch.

With the classy vibes of the decor, Greenhouse Canteen is a nice vegan option on the Gold Coast.
