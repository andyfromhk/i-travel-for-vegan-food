A collaboration with Good Town Donuts, The Little Bakery Tokyo offers a range of vegan treats located just off ‘Cat Street’ in Harajuku.

When available, you can find a good selection of fluffy vegan donuts (like matcha or passionfruit flavours), cinnamon rolls, baguettes, and other breads. They also serve oat milk lattes. However, the selection changes daily so there could be days with limited vegan options.

There are some dine-in seatings available, but because of the popularity of the cafe, expect to get the baked good as takeaway.
