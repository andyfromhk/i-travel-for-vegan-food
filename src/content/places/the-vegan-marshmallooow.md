Situated in the B2F food hall of Ginza Mitsukoshi, The Vegan Marshmallooow is a fully vegan bakery renowned for its stunningly beautiful and delicious cakes, cookies, and pastries. They boast one of Japan's largest vegan bakery selections, including gluten-free options and popular items like their 'anime-perfect' strawberry cream cake. Everything is crafted with refined textures and flavours. While items are pricey (often over ¥1000), many find the quality justifies the cost. Service is friendly. Keep in mind it's a counter within a busy food hall, and there's no immediate seating – you can take your treats up to the 9th-floor terrace.
