Vegan Ramen by Playpen Friends is a trendy vegan ramen restaurant in the heart of Osaka. They offer 3 types of ramen broth: miso, shoyu (soy sauce) and tomato.

While the portion of the toppings and ramen is generous, for me, the flavours are on the simpler side, which gives me a sense that this restaurant is tailoring the offerings to cater to the expectations of tourists looking for a vegan take on traditional ramen. Despite that, it’s still a decent choice given its central location and friendly service.
