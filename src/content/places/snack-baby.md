This gelato shop in Central surprises me with their high quality interesting flavours and high quality gelato. They clearly marked “AF” (Animal Free) for the vegan options, which are made with cashew milk. The vegan flavours span from classic choices like “intense chocolate” to inventive ones such as wasabi lime and Szechuan chilli mango.

I was particularly blown away by the pistachio crunch gelato - it’s so good that I went there twice just to have it again. It’s one of the best gelatos I’ve had with its full, rich pistachio flavour and satisfying crunchy pieces.

The space of Snack Baby is a retro vibe, with both indoors and outdoor seatings, though it gets super busy at night.
