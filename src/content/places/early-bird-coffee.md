Early Bird Coffee is a sweet and creative all-vegan cafe run by a kind, English-speaking couple. The menu offers a surprising range of options, with a special focus on pancakes that come in both sweet and savoury varieties, such as the unique falafel pancakes. In addition to pancakes, you can find lunch plates and baked goods. Be sure to check their Instagram for pop-up events and irregular hours, and keep in mind that they are cash only. To get there, it's a 15-minute walk from Kotoku-in.
