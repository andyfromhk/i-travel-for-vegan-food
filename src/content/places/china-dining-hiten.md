China Dining Hiten is a Chinese restaurant that serves meat but offers a separate menu with vegan options, making it a convenient choice for mixed-diet groups. Kepe in mind that the restaurant is on the 2nd floor and you may have to ask for the vegan menu. Popular choices include the mapo tofu and soy cashew stir-fry.
