Vegetarian Society near Chatuchak Market is a truly local experience. The food is tasty and cheap, but there are a few things you'll need to know beforehand for a smooth visit.

It's a canteen-style venue with vegan food vendors. The food isn't fancy, but it's flavourful and comforting. You can expect to find classic stir-fries, noodle soups, curries served over brown rice, and a lot of tasty mock meat dishes here.

Food stalls don't accept cash. Instead, head to the voucher counter on the right when you walk in to exchange your cash for food tickets. The food here is cheap, so you probably need fewer vouchers than you think. For your reference, the 3 plates of food I got cost only 100 baht. When ordering, there isn't much English on the menu, and staff may speak limited English. So be sure to learn a few simple phrases and point towards the dishes that catch your eye.

Once you've got your food, you grab your own cutlery from a station located at the centre of the canteen, and when you're done, you're expected to return your plates and utensils to the cleaning area.

The dishes I tried were all solid and tasty. My personal favourite was the skewers with grilled mushrooms and mock meat, especially with the spicy, tangy green dip it came with. The crispy fried taro cake was also delicious with a mix of crispy and chewy textures.
