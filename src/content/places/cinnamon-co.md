*(Cinnamon & Co. is permanently closed since Oct 2023)*

Cinnamon & Co is a great vegan restaurant cafe located in West End, Brisbane. I've tried a few dishes here and the quality is consistently high. They change the menu from time to time. Recently, I've tried the the bang bang bowl which is a rice bowl with sweet chilli sauce on top of crispy tofu and veggies. The tarragona and ianri are also my previous favourites that have rotated out of the menu at the time of writing. For drinks, try the house-brewed chai if you are a chai lover.

The environment at Cinnamon & Co is also cozy and welcoming. They have a basement level where they sometimes host a vintage clothing shop or activities like trivia or board game night. The service is also warm and friendly, which makes for a great dining experience.
