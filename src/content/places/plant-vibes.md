Plant Vibes is a beautiful vegan cafe in Bowen Hills. You'll be welcomed by the friendly staff and cozy decor when you arrive. The menu features classic cafe dishes like toasties and pancakes. They update their menu every few months.

On weekends, they often have special cabinet dessert items like injected croissants or coffee in doughnut.
