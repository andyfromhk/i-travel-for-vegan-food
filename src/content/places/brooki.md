Brooki Bakehouse is a popular Brisbane bakery known for its big, chunky cookies, brownies and cupcakes. Most of the menu isn't vegan, but they make a vegan version of their classic choc chip cookie.

I really like the texture of the vegan choc chip cookie. It's packed with chocolate chunks and tastes kind of buttery, which is impressive for a vegan cookie. The downsides are that the price is on the higher side for a single cookie, and the vegan option isn't always available. If you're making a special trip, it's worth checking with the store first.
