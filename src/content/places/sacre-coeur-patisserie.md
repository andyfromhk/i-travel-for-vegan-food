Sacre Coeur Patisserie is a fully vegan bakery that also offers excellent gluten-free options. All the baked goods are made in-house. Options include pistachio croissants, hazelnut pain au chocolat, and fruit tarts. Savoury options are also available like bolognese pie and cheesy sausage roll.

I tried the pistachio croissant, which was delicious, though slightly undercooked in the middle. There are some relaxing outdoor seating and the staff is friendly.
