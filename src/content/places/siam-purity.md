Siam Purity is a vegan Thai restaurant in the Pacific Fair shopping centre. You will find traditional dishes here like pad Thai, tom yum soup, curry rice and more. The menu is pretty extensive.

The food is well-made with reasonable prices. The crispy honey soy and green curry fried rice are both delicious.

One interesting things for this place is that the food is delivered by a friendly robot!
