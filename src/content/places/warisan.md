Warisan is a funky Indonesian restaurant and bar on a side street in the Valley, with a vegan section on the menu that has quite a few options. I tried a few dishes, and my favourites were the tempeh chips and the mie goreng tek-tek. The cap chay (stir-fried vegetables), however, came in an overly salty sauce.

**Tip**: Order the mie goreng tek-tek from the vegan section, as the regular version on the main menu comes with egg. If you're in a group, there's also a vegan banquet (minimum two people).
