Sōfu is a tofu gelato shop on Edward Street in the city, and the first of its kind in Brisbane. The vegan flavours are clearly marked. When I visited, they were original, black sesame and coconut pandan. The gelato is rich and creamy.

My only wish is that all the flavours were vegan, which should be quite doable for a tofu gelato shop.
