Vegan Cafe PQ's is a gem offering a welcoming, chill vibe and highly creative, beautifully presented 100% vegan food. They use quality organic and fair-trade ingredients for their daily changing lunch sets and impressive seasonal menus (like Sakura-themed dishes). Standouts include hamburger steak, sweet and savory pancakes, and delicious baked goods. The cafe is spacious, peaceful, and notably dog-friendly (they even have a dog menu!). Located slightly out of the way but considered well worth the trip, it's perfect for a relaxing meal. Note: Ordering is via QR code, and they have specific opening days/times (closed Tue/Wed, Wed is baked goods/drinks only).
