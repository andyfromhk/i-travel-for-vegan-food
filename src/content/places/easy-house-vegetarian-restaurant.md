It’s rare to find an authentic yum cha place nowadays, let alone an all-vegan one. Easy House ticks all the boxes: vegan, authentic and delicious.

They do yum cha at lunch and dinner times. The dishes I've tried are exceptional with authentic taste. The flavours are true to what you will get in Hong Kong. Other than the yum cha, they also do other classic dishes that are commonly found in Hong Kong.

The staff in this family-owned restaurant is very friendly and helpful.

Highly recommend Easy House if you want to try good quality vegan yum cha.
