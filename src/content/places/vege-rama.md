A convenient spot for vegans in Brisbane's CBD, this all-vegan spot offers a satisfying hot food counter experience.

When you enter, go to the back of the store to select from a rotating array of curries, daal, pakora, vegan lasagne, and more – the Thai green curry and stroganoff are my favourites when available. Beyond the rice-and-dish combos, you can also find dosa, burgers, and salads. While they also have vegan cakes and desserts, their texture can lean towards 'jelly-like' rather than creamy.
