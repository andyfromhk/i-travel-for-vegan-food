Premium SOW is a fully vegan fine bakery and patisserie specializing in exquisite French-style cakes, gelato, parfaits, cookies, and chocolates. The gelato, particularly flavors like tiramisu and pistachio, receives high praise for its rich taste, as do the various tarts. While the offerings are beautifully crafted and delicious, be prepared for high prices and potentially small portions, making it more suitable for special occasions or gifts. The space itself is very small, possibly standing-room only. Note that many items contain coconut, though some coconut-free options exist. Also, gelato "doubles" feature a smaller second scoop, and some flavors cost extra.
