Mercy Vegan Factory is one of my favourite places in Osaka because of the amazing food and comfy , neighbourhood vibe. I love their vegan egg sandwich – the vegan egg salad part is realistic and delicious, and it's served on soft sandwich bread. This tasty egg sandwich is also available for takeaway if you're in a rush.

You'll also find comfort food like karaage chicken, Korean gimbap and curry rice in their lunch and dinner menus. In the cabinet, they carry a good range of vegan desserts and patisserie using seasonal fruits. All the food I've tried here is delicious and well-made.

The closest station to Mercy Vegan Factory is Tanimachi 9-chome Station, but it's walkable from Dotonbori or Namba Station.
