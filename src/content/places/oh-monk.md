Oh Monk is a modern Southeast Asian restaurant in leafy Tarragindi with quite a lot of vegan options. I like that the vegan dishes are cheaper than the meat versions. For example, a tofu stir-fry is $21, while the meat stir-fries are $24–28.

I tried two of the lunch specials: the green curry tofu rice bowl ($15) and the crispy cauliflower Japanese curry rice bowl ($17). Both were good value and tasted okay.
