Located next to the Sumida River, Marugoto Vegan Dining Asakusa (formerly The Farm Cafe) is a cozy vegan cafe that's walking distance from Senso-Ji Temple.

They offer a variety of delicious options, including tempura, fried tempeh, curry, salads, and waffles. Go for the lunch plate, which features a mix of curry, tempura, and salad, if you want to try a bit of everything. I chose the tempura curry rice which includes a variety of veggies with a tasty curry sauce. Staff are warm and welcoming, and their handwritten menu has English translation.
