This vegan bakery and patisserie is located near the Southbank train station. Although it only has a takeaway counter and no seats during my visit, I'm impressed with the quality of the snickerdoodle cookie croissant I tried. It tastes amazing with all the wondering layers of a good croissant. Other than croissant, you can expect to find danishes, cookies, and some savoury options.
