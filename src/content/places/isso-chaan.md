Isso is a beautiful and intimate restaurant offering a fusion of vegan Taiwanese and Japanese traditional cuisine. With a peaceful, zen-like atmosphere, it’s a place that makes you slow down and enjoy the moment. Remember to take your shoes off when you enter. The menu is fully vegan, centred around Taiwanese-Japanese fusion dishes. Try their Isso plate to sample a variety of beautifully presented dishes.
