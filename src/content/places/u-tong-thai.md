U Tong Thai is a hidden gem in Brisbane that specialises in vegan Thai food. The dishes are in general well portioned and delicious. All savoury options I've had here are great. Some stand-out dishes include the whole coconut curry, pad see ew, pineapple fried rice and the side of coconut rice.

Free parking is available at the front of the restaurants.
