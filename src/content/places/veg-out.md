This all-vegan cafe gives you a beautiful view of the Kamo River while you dine. If you come early, you may even be able to secure one of the window seats.

The menu offers classic Japanese cafe dishes like lunch plates, curry rice and buddha bowl. I tried the Veg Out plate which comes with a selection of small dishes made with organic veggies and grains from local farmers. Flavours could be on the milder side for some people. The preparation of ingredients is relatively simple which showcases the natural flavours of the local veggies. They also have a selection of desserts including the pecan tart I tried, but it was a bit too dry and crumbly for my liking.

Even though some of the dishes may not wow you, Veg Out still offers a unique dining experience with a beautiful river view.
