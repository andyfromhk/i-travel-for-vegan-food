Located near Shibuya Crossing, Bio Cafe combines a French bakery with a relaxed, organic-focused cafe. While it serves meat, there are clearly marked vegan options available on the English menu. You can find dishes like soy cheese carbonara and tofu steak mochi brown rice. The portion could be a bit small for the relatively high prices.
