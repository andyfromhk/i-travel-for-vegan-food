This cozy, vegan restaurant offers a delicious fusion of Japanese and Middle Eastern dishes. The cafe has a relaxing vibe decorated with wood and dried flowers. The cafe often is run by just the owner, both taking your order and cooking up food. The standout menu item is the excellent falafel, which you can enjoy in a pita or as part of a larger plate. An English menu with photos is available.
