Pivot BASE is a fully vegan cafe and bar conveniently located in the busy Dotonbori area. Their concept is to offer vegan versions of iconic Osaka specialty street foods, such as takoyaki (octopus balls), okonomiyaki (savoury pancakes) and kushikatsu (deep-fried skewers). The menu also includes dishes like yakisoba, ramen, vegan cutlet rice bowls (katsudon), and fried gyoza. English menu is available and you can communicate with the staff in English.
