Located conveniently near the popular Senso-ji Temple, Hatoya is a dedicated vegan shop specialising in Japanese fruit sandwiches (fruit sando). They use vegan whipped cream and bread, filling them with a variety of seasonal fruits like strawberries, pineapple and mandarin.

The red bean paste and strawberry sandwich one I tried was delicious with light and creamy whipped cream, and sweet strawberries. They have a few seats located at the store for you to eat the sandwich there, but it's more of a takeaway place. Nonetheless, Hatoya is a great option to try out the trendy fruit sandwich at a convenient location.
