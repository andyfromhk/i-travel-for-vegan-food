Located on the 2nd floor of Laforet Harajuku, OVGO B.A.K.E.R. is a fully vegan cookie shop and cafe with a cute atmosphere. They offer a wide variety of American-style cookies, alongside scones, ice cream, cookie sundaes, and coffee/tea. It provides a convenient vegan dessert option within the busy mall. However, reviews are quite mixed. While some enjoy the scones or appreciate the vegan availability, many visitors report the cookies being extremely hard and difficult to eat, and the coffee quality has been criticized. Some also find it pricey for what's offered. It might be worth a stop if you're nearby and need a quick vegan treat, but manage expectations, especially regarding the cookie texture. Credit card only.
