Tucked in the Post Office Square food court in Brisbane CBD, Happy Dough offers quite a good variety of vegan-friendly doughnuts.

Their base cinnamon doughnut is both vegan and gluten-free. Other than that, you'll find a good range of vegan-friendly glazed doughnut options, with tempting flavours like vegan caramel, custard, and 'new-tella'.
