I recently stopped by Lord of the Fries in Fortitude Valley after quite some time, and I’m glad to say their vegan fast food is still hitting the mark. The portions are generous, the flavours are good, and it’s clear they’re sticking to what they do best - delivering solid vegan comfort food.

I tried the Biggie, their take on a vegan Big Mac. With two thick burger patties and all the classic burger ingredients, it’s a satisfying and fulfilling meal on its own. The sweet potato fries (freshly made and also in a generous portion) and cookies & cream milkshake (a bit coconut-y in flavour but overall creamy and tasty) are both great choices to add to your vegan comfort food meal.
