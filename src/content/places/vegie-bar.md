Vegie Bar is a long-standing and popular vegetarian cafe in Fitzroy. It's known for its large, diverse menu that includes curries, pizzas, burgers, noodles, and salads. The menu is vegan by default with optional dairy for some dishes. Portions are generally generous, and it’s often busy. It's a reliable choice for a casual meal in the Fitzroy area.
