Located in West End's West Village, this popular gelateria often draws long queues after dinner.

All sorbet options are clearly marked as vegan, including the surprisingly creamy and rich cookie sorbet packed with Oreo bites. About 30-40% of the offerings are vegan-friendly. Staff is happy to offer samples to help you choose. The cones are vegan-friendly too.
