Manna Haven is a not-for-profit vegan cafe run by the Byron Bay Seventh-day Adventist Church, and it has been feeding locals for over 20 years. It's a lunch spot with a counter of hot and cold dishes that changes regularly, so you can mix and match mains, salads and desserts. It's only open for weekday lunch, so plan around it. Eat in the indoor area or the garden, or grab your food to go.

I had lunch here and tried the lasagna and the frittata. The frittata (more like a quiche) was nice and loaded with pumpkin, and the staff offered to heat it up for me. The lasagna tasted good but was a bit too salty for my taste. There's no coffee here, but there are smoothies, a house chai made on rooibos, hot chocolate and herbal teas (the banana smoothie is listed with honey, so ask for it without).
