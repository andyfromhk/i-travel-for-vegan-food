If you're looking for a truly local and authentic Thai vegan experience in Phuket Town, Ruamjai Vegan Restaurant on Ranong Road is a great destination. It's a busy, no-frills spot that feels like a real community.

When you walk in, you're greeted by a huge counter loaded with dozens of different dishes. There's no menu and very limited English, so definitely come prepared with a few useful Thai phrases or just point to the dishes that catch your eye.

After ordering, pay at the cashier in the middle of the restaurant, and then find a seat. It's lively, a bit chaotic, and an experience that is truly local.
