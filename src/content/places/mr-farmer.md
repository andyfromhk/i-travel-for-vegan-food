Located in the upscale Midtown Hibiya complex, Mr. Farmer is a health-oriented cafe serving American-style dishes with meat, but offering clearly marked vegan and gluten-free options. You can find satisfying vegan choices like Buddha bowls (the tofu comes recommended), wraps, burgers served with potato, and their popular locomoco bowl. They also offer smoothies, juices, and complimentary flavoured water. The interior is nice, service is fast, and ordering via QR code (in English) is convenient. While it's a good option for mixed groups in a fancy area, be aware that many find it quite pricey, and portion sizes might seem modest to some.
