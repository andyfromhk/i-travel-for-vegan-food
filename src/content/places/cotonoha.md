Just a short walk from Hase station, Cotonoha is an all-vegan restaurant set in a vintage Japanese house with a cute patio. The food here focuses on fresh, healthy ingredients. The menu is concise and typically features their buddha bowl plate and curry plate, which are beautifully presented and delicious. Although the friendly owners may not speak great English, you'll find an English menu.
