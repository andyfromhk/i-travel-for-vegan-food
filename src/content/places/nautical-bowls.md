Located in West Village shopping centre, Nautical Bowls specialises in colourful smoothie bowls and açai bowls. Almost all the bowls are vegan, but double check as some uses honey as a topping. Apart from their bowls being colourful and refreshing, a feature I love about them is their nut butter machine, which makes freshly ground nut butter for the bowls.
