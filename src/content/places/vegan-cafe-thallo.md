Thallo is an all-vegan cafe with a comfy interior and a passion for animal rights, run by a friendly owner who is also involved in cat rescue. The menu changes regularly, but you can expect a colourful plate using seasonal produce and soy products. They may also have desserts like chocolate cakes. Keep in mind that they only open on the weekends.
