Go Vegan Cafe is a super cozy and comfortable spot in Rawai that feels part wellness centre, part home-style cafe. They have a welcoming space with both indoor (be ready to take off your shoes) and outdoor seating, and they even host yoga and sound healing classes.

The menu is centred around healthy and nourishing food, with everything from smoothie bowls and wraps to heartier options like pasta and pizza. While I'd heard amazing things, my food experience was a bit mixed. I decided to try the chick-fil-A burger, but the fried tofu patty didn't have much flavour, and the batter wasn't crispy. What saved the meal for me was the tea rose coffee, which mixes rose syrup with oat milk and coffee and tastes like a Turkish delight latte.
