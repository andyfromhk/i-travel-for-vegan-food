Funky Pies is a vegan gem, walking distance from the famous Bondi Beach, which makes it a perfect spot for vegans travelling there. They specialises in chunky, handmade pies – all vegan, with gluten-free options available for some popular choices like the Funky Chunky and Moroccan Tangine. They serve your pie traditionally with mash, gravy, and peas, or opt for a side salad.

Beyond pies, you may also find some cakes, cupcakes, and banana bread on-site and serve organic fairtrade coffee (with Bonsoy), juices, and vegan shakes. It’s a great spot to refuel after hitting the beach or the Coogee-Bondi walk. They also have a small selection of vegan groceries to take home.
