Located in Paddington, Nain serves up impressive Middle Eastern and Mediterranean cuisine with a separate vegan menu. The chefs clearly put a lot of thought into creating dishes with well-executed flavours and textures, and the menu changes seasonally.

Standout vegan dishes might include creative share plates like "Muhamarra" with an interesting flavour mix of black tahini and cashew cheese, or hearty mains such as a Fable mushroom lasagna that goes great with the rich cashew parmesan and sundried tomato. While the food quality is consistently high, keep in mind that the prices are also relatively high, and service can sometimes be on the slower side, with waits between courses.
