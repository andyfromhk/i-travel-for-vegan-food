This ramen shop is a convenient option for vegans, located directly across from Nara Park and Nara National Museum. Unlike their [sister shop in Osaka](/restaurants/vegan-ramen-by-playpen-friends) which is all-vegan, this branch in Nara serves meat, but still offer clearly marked vegan options like vegan miso ramen and vegan tonkotsu ramen.
