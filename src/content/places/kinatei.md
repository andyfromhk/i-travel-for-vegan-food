A vegetarian cafe 10-minute walk from JR Nara Station, offering daily lunch plates with 4 small dishes, soup and rice. The small dishes often include salad, steamed vegetables, and fried tofu. They also serve ramen or udon sets. Lunch sets are 2,000 yen.

Some dishes may have a dairy and dairy-free option. Opens for lunch, but the dinner kaiseki cuisine requires reservations.
