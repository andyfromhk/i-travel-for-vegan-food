Ballon is a tiny, fully vegan eatery near Nakameguro station, focused primarily on falafel and soft-serve ice cream. Their falafel, served in sandwiches or bowls, receives high praise for its exceptional flavour. They also offer tasty soups (like tomato) and salads. It's a great spot for a quick, delicious Israeli-inspired meal or a vegan soft serve treat. The main thing to keep in mind is the extremely limited seating – only about four small tables. While service is fast, portion sizes for meals are described as medium, so factor that in if you're very hungry. Check closing times as they vary slightly.
