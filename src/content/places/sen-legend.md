This Vietnamese fusion restaurant has a dedicated vegan menu. With prior experience of running a vegetarian grocery shop (Health T) and another vegan-friendly eatery in West End (Fat Carrot), they know how to cook a flavourful and satisfying vegan meal.

This restaurant has a dedicated vegan menu with classic Vietnamese dishes like pho and rice paper rolls. All the dishes I tried here are well-made and flavourful.
