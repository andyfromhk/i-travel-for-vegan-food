Felix for Goodness is a popular cafe tucked away in Brisbane CBD's Burnett Lane, known for its relaxed, urban vibe. It gets quite busy during lunch, and the tables are fairly close together.

The menu changes regularly and features clearly marked vegan (VG) and vegan-possible (VGO) options – just keep in mind that substitutions may not be available for VGO dishes. While some may find it on the expensive side, the food is tasty. I've tried the surprisingly delicious vegan tuna bruschetta, which uses chickpeas with seaweed as the 'tuna', alongside fried tofu, roasted tomatoes, and standout pickled cucumber. Other options include cafe classics like granola or falafel bowls. Service is typically swift and friendly, though you have to order at the counter.
