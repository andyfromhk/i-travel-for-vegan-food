This Italian eatery in West End, Brisbane offer some classic dishes including pizza, pasta and tiramisu. The food is delicious in general. I especially love the pizzas and the tiramisu. The portion of the pasta dishes, however, could be a little bit small for the price.

Also keep in mind that they charge for table water. Ask for tap water if you don't want to pay for water.
