Shift Eatery is a fully vegan deli cafe in Surry Hills, particularly known for making its own vegan meats, cheeses, and sauces in-house. The menu covers familiar cafe favourites, offering all-day breakfast options like omelettes and pancakes, alongside a variety of sandwiches, toasties, salads, and bowls.

Try their crispy 'chicken' burger and flavourful vegan omelette. They also serve smoothies, coffee, and display sweet treats in their cabinet. The vibe is friendly and unfussy, typical of a neighbourhood spot, though the cafe itself is small with limited seating. It's a reliable choice for a casual meal, takeaway, or delivery. You can also purchase their house-made deli products and other vegan groceries to take home.
