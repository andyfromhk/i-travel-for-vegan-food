This tea shop in West End has quite a few vegan dessert options. The best one being their vegan soft serve ice cream. Flavour rotates regularly, and they include cherry blossom, chai, taro and more. You can add additional toppings like pearls, Oreo and fruits. They also have vegan bubble tea and sometimes other vegan desserts. The prices are reasonable with generous portion.
