Moofree Burger is a vegan burger chain in the Brisbane area. This shop in North Lakes is shared with Charlie's Raw Squeeze - A smoothie and juice bar.

They have a wide selection of burgers, chips, desserts and drinks. In general the food is well-made and tasty. However, sometimes the food is not executed perfectly, like overly salty chips.

They run discount on various items throughout the week, like $10 burgers on Monday and buy-1-get-1 free smoothie on Tuesday.
