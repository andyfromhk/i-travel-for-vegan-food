YOMG Burgers is a great spot for vegan-friendly eats if you’re craving a combo of burger with froyo. Though it’s an omni restaurant, they’ve got some solid vegan options, and I went there specifically to try their Miss Fu vegan burger and vegan froyo flavours.

The Miss Fu burger was the only vegan burger option they have. You can choose between a plantein patty or a vegetable fritter - I prefer the fritter, which is a bit more substantial with a great texture compared to the plantein one which is a bit too thin for the burger. The burger also comes with a vibrant red beetroot bun, crispy fried potato, vegan cheese, tofu mayo, and more.

For their frozen yoghurt, they had three vegan flavours on the day of visit: salted pistachio, chocolate fudge, and açai. All of them are great but pistachio is my favourite. Unlike the yoghurt, the toppings aren't marked whether they are vegan, but the staff was super helpful in checking which ones were suitable. Go for the classic combo of Biscoff sauce, Oreos, fruits, and nuts for a satisfying bowl of froyo.

While the burger and froyo were excellent, it’s worth noting that their vegan options beyond these are somewhat limited. They do offer sweet potato fries and 'super blend' smoothies, but oddly, their "just fries" aren’t vegan because they contain dairy.
