This unique spot is a fully vegan gyoza house and coffee shop, serving up pan-fried or steamed dumplings bursting with distinct flavours – so flavourful, reviewers say no sauce is needed! Each gyoza in their mixed platters tastes different, with fillings like shiitake, miso ginger, and kimchi being favourites. The gyoza wrappers are noted as being slightly thicker than typical. They also offer great coffee, beers, and fun vegan cream sodas. It's a charming but popular place, so expect a potential wait, especially on weekends. Located in a quiet neighborhood, it's worth the trip for dedicated gyoza fans. Closed Mon/Tue.
