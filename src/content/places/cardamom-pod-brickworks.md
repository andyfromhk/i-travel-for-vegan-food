Cardamom Pod Brickworks is a modern cafe is located in Brickworks Ferry Rd, Southport. Even though the food I've tried there is well-made and delicious, it's important to note that they have introduced dairy cheese to their otherwise vegan menu in 2024.

You will find some familiar dishes that are elevated. All the dishes are as beautiful as they are delicious. I really appreciate the presentation and effort they put in the dishes. In the times I've visited, the food quality is very consistently high. For first-timers, try their beautiful acai bowls or blinged avo benny. If you visit on the weekend, the Indian street food waffle is a great choice.

They are usually quite busy especially on weekends so be prepared to wait a little bit for a table.
