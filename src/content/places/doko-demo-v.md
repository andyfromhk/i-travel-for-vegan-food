Doko Demo V is a 100% vegan cafe in Burleigh Waters on the Gold Coast. Its menu is diverse with Southeast-Asian dishes like Pad Thai and Okonomiyaki to Western favourites such as burgers, pizzas, and pasta. Popular dishes include their Taipei 101 burger, fish and chips, and the sizzling Mongolian beef. The food quality is good with big portions.The vibe is relaxed with Bali-inspired bamboo and greenery decor.
