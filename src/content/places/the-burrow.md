This bar & restaurant in West End has funky atmosphere and colourful eclectic furniture. Their menu has some clearly-marked vegan options including pizzas, burger and nachos.

I've tried two of their vegan pizzas: Brother from another mother and Tim Curry III. The base is more of a thin cracker type base. They are generous with the topping. However, the taste is rather mild.

Overall good choice for vegan dinner because of the unique vibes and friendly staff.
