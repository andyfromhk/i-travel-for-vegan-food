Fundies is a long-running health food and organic grocery store on Byron's main street. It's a good place to stock up on vegan snacks, plant milks and organic produce for your stay.

It also has a separate garden cafe and fresh food section with vegan options like salads, burgers, brownies and more. I didn't try anything from the cafe on this trip, but it's handy if you want to grab something quick or put together a beach picnic.
