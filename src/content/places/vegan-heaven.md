Vegan Heaven is a local spot that welcomes you with its colourful orange and green decor. They offer a huge menu, including Western breakfast options like all-day breakfast and BLT sandwich, but I was there for a more traditional Thai dinner and was not disappointed.

You'll find classic Thai dishes here. I opted for the fresh tempeh spring rolls, which were packed with savoury fried tempeh and veggies, as well as a tangy sesame dip. For my main, the penang curry is a good choice with its strong lemongrass flavour and a nice tanginess from kaffir lime. The clear soup was surprisingly flavourful, light, and sweet. For dessert, the deep-fried banana was crispy like a spring roll on the outside and filled with warm, soft banana, served with maple syrup.
