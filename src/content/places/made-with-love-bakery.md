This bakery in Southport is all vegan with a dazzling range of baked goods. They offer bakery classics like filled doughnuts, croissants, cream jam buns, as well as some more creative options like croffin (croissant muffin). You’ll also find savoury options here like cheesy garlic scroll, pizza bread and a selection of hot pies.

It’s hard to pick a favourite out of everything I tried here, but my favourites were the choc honeycomb croffin and the cinnamon scroll with biscoff icing.

Although the food was great, I wouldn’t really recommend the drinks. Both the iced chocolate and the dirty chai I tried were pretty weak unfortunately.
