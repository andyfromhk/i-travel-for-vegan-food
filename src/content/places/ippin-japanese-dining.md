Ippin is a high-end Japanese restaurant upstairs in West Village. A full dinner here is a splurge, but the express lunch set is a great way to try it. One of the set options is the "Vegetarian Set", which comes with salad, sushi, tempura, miso eggplant, agedashi tofu and more. It tasted pretty good, and it's a lovely spot for a special lunch.

**Tip**: The Vegetarian Set isn't vegan by default, so make sure you tell the staff you're vegan when ordering. For dinner, Ippin also has a multi-course vegetarian set menu that must be pre-ordered by email at least a day ahead; mention that you're vegan when you book.
