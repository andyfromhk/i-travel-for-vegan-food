(This review was written for their previous Glebe shop.) In Glebe, you'll find the Vegan Grocery Store - An all-vegan supermarket with a great range of products. It's located right opposite two other vegan favourites – Soul Burger and Oh My Days. From tasty cheeses and meat substitutes to vegan leather handbags and pet foods, they have got it all.

Honestly, it's hard to walk around without wanting to buy everything. Be sure to go to their special section to pick up a few discounted goodies. And if you can’t make it in person or want to shop from home, they have an online store that delivers Australia-wide.
