**_Hey there, I'm Andy!_** Some of you may know me as [@andyfromhk](https://www.instagram.com/andyfromhk/). Welcome to my corner of the internet: I Travel For Vegan Food.

For me, food is a huge part of the travel experience, and travel is my passion. Combine those with a commitment to veganism, and you get ... well, me! And this blog! I'm a vegan foodie and photographer who believes that exploring the world should always involve great meals.

I still remember the planning involved in my first few trips as a vegan (stressful!). Finding truly *good* vegan food – the kind that really make a trip memorable – felt hard to achieve back then. So I started documenting everything: the must-try restaurants, the hidden gems, the dishes that I still remember to this day. Soon, **_I Travel For Vegan Food_** was born.

This blog is my way of sharing my findings with you. Because I know the joy that comes from really great food, especially when you're traveling. My goal is to provide the information and inspiration you need to plan your own dream foodie trips.

Thanks for stopping by. Let's explore the world one vegan meal at a time!

Andy
