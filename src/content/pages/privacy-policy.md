*I Travel For Vegan Food*

# Privacy Policy

Last updated: Oct 1st, 2026

## About this policy

This policy explains what information I Travel For Vegan Food collects when you use itravelforveganfood.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Questions about privacy: email [hello@itravelforveganfood.com](mailto:hello@itravelforveganfood.com).

## The short version

- We don't sell or rent your personal information, and you don't need an account to use this site.
- Your saved places stay in your own browser. They only reach us if you ask us to email them to you.
- We only use your location when you tap a button that needs it, and it stays in your browser.
- We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, and Google Search Console to see how it appears in search.
- If you join our newsletter, we send it with Kit, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

## What we collect and why

### When you browse the site

- **Site statistics.** Google Analytics records how the site is used: pages viewed, time on page, the site that sent you, your device and browser type, and your approximate location (country or city). We use this to see which guides are useful and what to improve. It doesn't tell us your name or contact details.
- **Technical information.** Like every website, the companies that host and deliver our pages see standard technical details when your device asks for a page, such as your IP address and browser type. They use them to deliver the site and keep it secure.

### Saved places and preferences

When you save a place or guide, it's stored in your own browser on your device, along with small preferences like your currency for the price converter. This information isn't sent to us. See "Cookies and storage on your device" below.

### "Email me my list"

If you ask us to email your saved list to you:

- **What we receive:** your email address and the items in your list (place and guide names, their cities and their links).
- **What we use it for:** creating and sending that one email.
- **Bot check:** Cloudflare Turnstile checks your browser for signs of automated abuse before the email is sent.
- **Abuse prevention:** we briefly use your IP address and the email address to limit how often lists can be sent.
- **What we keep:** we don't keep a copy of your list or your address after the email is sent. Our email provider, Cloudflare, keeps a delivery record (such as the recipient address and whether the email was delivered) for a limited time.
- **Mailing lists:** you're only added to our newsletter if you tick the box asking for new guides.

### Our newsletter

If you tick "Also send me new vegan guides" or sign up with the newsletter form on our pages, we add your email address to our newsletter, which we send using Kit. The sign-up form on our pages is handled by Webflow, our website host, which stores the submission. When you join from your saved list, we also note the cities in it (for example "Saved: Tokyo"), so we can send you guides that are relevant to your trip. Kit records standard newsletter statistics, such as whether an email was opened. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.

### Your location

"My location" on map guides and "Distance from Me" on destination pages use your device's location, but only after you tap them and your browser asks for your permission. Your location is used inside your browser to show distances and sort places. It isn't sent to us or stored.

If you tap **Directions**, your location is included in the link that opens Google Maps, so Google can plan the route. Google's privacy policy then applies.

### Buying a book

Our travel books are sold through Gumroad, which handles checkout and payment. Gumroad shares your name, email address and order details with us so we can deliver your purchase and help if anything goes wrong. We never see your full payment details.

### Affiliate links

Some links on the site are affiliate links, for example to Trip.com, Klook, GetYourGuide, Booking.com, Expedia, Viator and Airalo. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. These companies may use cookies on their own sites to record that you came from us. We don't receive your personal details from them, only sales totals.

## Cookies and storage on your device

**Cookies.** Google Analytics uses cookies to tell visits apart, for example whether you've been here before. Instagram posts and the sites behind affiliate links may set their own cookies.

**Browser storage.** Our site features keep a few items in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device:

Item - What it holds (Sent to us?)

- `itfvf:saved - `Your saved places and guides (Only when you use "Email me my list")
- `itfvf:email - `The address you last used for "Email me my list", to fill in the form next time (Only when you send a list)
- `itfvf:home-currency - `Your currency for the price converter (No)
- `itfvf:rates - `Exchange rates, refreshed every 12 hours (No)

**Clearing it.** Tap **Clear all** in your Saved list to remove saved places, or clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove everything.

## How long we keep information

- Your saved list and address for "Email me my list" (Not kept by us after the email is sent. Cloudflare's delivery record is kept for a limited time)

## Your choices and rights

### Things you can do yourself

- **Newsletter:** unsubscribe with the link at the bottom of any newsletter email.
- **Saved list:** tap **Clear all** in your Saved list, or clear this site's data in your browser.
- **Location:** your browser always asks first. You can change or remove the permission in your browser settings at any time.
- **Analytics:** install the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout), or block cookies in your browser settings.

### Asking us

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email [hello@itravelforveganfood.com](mailto:hello@itravelforveganfood.com) and we'll reply within 30 days.

**In the European Union or the United Kingdom,** you also have the right to restrict or object to how we use your information, to receive a copy of it in a portable format, and to withdraw your consent at any time. We rely on:

- your **consent** for the newsletter and your location;
- your **request** when we send you your saved list;
- our **legitimate interest** in running, securing and improving the site, for site statistics and abuse prevention.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children, international transfers and security

**Children.** This site isn't aimed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has given us their details, email us and we'll delete them.

**International transfers.** The services we use are based in the United States and other countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live. We use established providers that protect personal information under their own privacy commitments and legal safeguards.

**Security.** The site uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our service keys are stored privately, never in the website's code, and the "Email me my list" feature uses a bot check and sending limits to prevent misuse. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

## Changes and contact

We'll update this page when our features or services change, and change the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your email address, we'll tell newsletter subscribers too.

Any questions or requests: [hello@itravelforveganfood.com](mailto:hello@itravelforveganfood.com).
