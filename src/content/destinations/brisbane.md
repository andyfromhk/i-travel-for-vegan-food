Brisbane, the sunny capital of Queensland, welcomes visitors with its laid-back charm and lively riverside energy. Explore the green **_South Bank Parklands_**, stroll along the **_Brisbane River_**, or take in the views from the **_Story Bridge_**. This city blends city style with a relaxed outdoor lifestyle.

For vegan travellers, Brisbane offers some wonderful food surprises waiting to be discovered. Indulge in comforting Thai-fusion comfort food from Vega Cafe, or slurp down a satisfyingly warm bowl of ramen from Neon Ramen. The city's growing vegan scene is ready to be explored.
