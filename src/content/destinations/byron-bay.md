Byron Bay, the laid-back coastal town on the far north coast of New South Wales, draws visitors with its golden beaches, surf culture and relaxed, bohemian spirit. Walk up to the Cape Byron Lighthouse on mainland Australia's most easterly point.

For vegan travellers, Byron Bay is a small town with plenty of delicious options. Treat yourself to an all-vegan dinner and one of the best tiramisus around at No Bones, or cool off with a scoop of matcha macadamia gelato from Frankies. The town's health-conscious vibe means vegan food is never far away.
