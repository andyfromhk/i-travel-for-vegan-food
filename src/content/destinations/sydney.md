Sydney, Australia's amazing harbour city, instantly impresses with its famous landmarks like the **_Sydney Opera House_** and **_Harbour Bridge_**. From the busy city centre to the popular shores of **_Bondi Beach_**, Sydney offers a stunning mix of natural beauty and big-city style.

The vegan food scene in Sydney is equally lively and exciting. Grab a slice of authentic Neapolitan-style pizza from Gigi Pizzeria, or dive into creative, modern Mexican flavours at Vandal Taqueria or Calle Rey. Sydney serves up world-class vegan dining with style.
