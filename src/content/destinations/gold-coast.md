The Gold Coast, famous for its sun-kissed beaches and sparkling coastline, is a playground of sun, surf, and fun. Beyond the well-known Surfers Paradise and exciting theme parks, discover the beautiful greenery of the nearby hinterland. It's a destination that radiates energy and cool coastal vibes.

Vegan food lovers will find exciting options here too, blending health-consciousness with flavour. Start your day with a beautiful, art-like acai bowl from Cardamom Pod, or treat yourself to authentic and satisfying vegan yum cha at Easy House.
