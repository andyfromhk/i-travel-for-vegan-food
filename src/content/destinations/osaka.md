Osaka is a vibrant and lively city that promises an unforgettable experience for vegan travellers. From the iconic **_Osaka Castle_** to the bustling **_Dotonbori district_**, this city has a lot for you to explore and experience. The friendly locals and their love for good food will make you feel welcomed.

Osaka is often refers to as the “Kitchen of Japan”, and that applies to vegan food as well. Vegan foodies will be delighted by the diverse plant-based offerings in Osaka. Vegancafe Sister is a must-visit for those with a sweet tooth. Or try the realistic and nostalgic egg sandwich from Mercy Vegan Factory.
