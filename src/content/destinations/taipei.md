Taipei is an exciting destination for vegan foodies. Not only is it a bustling city that offers a great blend of tradition with modernity, you can also find some of the best vegan food here in Taipei. Indulge in exquisite, creative dishes from classy restaurants like Foldie and Vegan Amore, or enjoy irresistible street food like dumplings and stinky tofu, Taipei has so much to offer in terms of excellent vegan food. In this section, let me take you through all the best vegan options in this vibrant city.
