Himeji is a beautiful, historic city not too far from Osaka and Kyoto, making it a great day trip idea for those who want to be immersed in Japan's rich history and culture. The city’s crown jewel is **_Himeji Castle_**, one of Japan's best preserved old castles. Also known as the "White Heron Castle”, visiting is a must to see its elegant, white appearance. Even though Himeji has lots of traditional sights, it also has some surprisingly amazing vegan restaurants like Sai Cafe and il riccio. With its unforgettable blend of history, beautiful gardens and delicious vegan food, Himeji is a great city to slow down, relax and savour the charms of this cultural gem.
