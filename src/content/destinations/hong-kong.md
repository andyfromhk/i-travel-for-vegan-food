Hong Kong, an energetic city where East truly meets West, amazes with its bright skyline and non-stop energy. From the busy street markets to the peaceful views from Victoria Peak, this city offers a captivating blend of tradition and modern life, set against the stunning background of Victoria Harbour.

For vegan adventurers, you'll be happy to hear that Hong Kong has some amazing vegan options. Experience traditional Cantonese flavours with delicious vegan dim sum at Veggie Kingdom, try out a vegan take on the popular egg waffle from LN Coffee, or stroll along bustling shopping centres with a bubble tea from Mother Pearl.
