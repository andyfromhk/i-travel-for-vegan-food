Tokyo, the pulsating heart of Japan, is a city that offers both rich Japanese culture and modern innovations. From the neon-lit streets of **_Kabukicho_** and iconic landmarks like the **_Sensoji Temple_** and the **_Tokyo Tower_**, Tokyo captivates travellers with its energy and contrasts.

For vegan foodies, Tokyo is an unexpected vegan food heaven. You can expect to find a dazzling array of vegan options in Tokyo, including the authentic, ultra-umami ramen from Vegan Bistro Jangara, realistic and meaty karaage from Izakaya Masaka, and the trendy whipped cream fruit sandwich from Fruits and Season.
