One of my favourite cities, Kyoto offers a harmonious blend of cultural richness and delectable vegan cuisines. Immerse yourself in the serenity of the golden **_Kinkaku-ji Temple_**, marvel at the stunning torii gates of **_Fushimi Inari Taisha_**, or stroll through the bamboo forest in **_Arashiyama_**.

When it comes to food, vegan foodies will be glad to know that there are gems waiting to be discovered throughout the city. Enjoy the lunch plate from Mumokuteki Cafe which comes with a dazzling selection of small dishes, or try out the viral ‘flower bouquet’ ice cream from (Thisis)Nature.
