While famous for its stunning beaches and turquoise waters, the island of Phuket is a destination for vegan foodies best known for hosting the world's most impressive vegan food celebration, the Phuket Vegetarian Festival. Beyond this incredible event, you'll find a thriving year-round scene, including high-quality international restaurants like Ubuntu Eat, or a luxurious plant-based resort like Soul Villa.
