Kobe is a lively port city located conveniently close to Osaka, about a 40-minute train ride away without needing to take the Shinkansen bullet train. Despite its compact size, this little city packs in plenty of attractions, like iconic **_Kobe Port Tower_**, the **_Harborland_** shopping district and the beautiful **_Ikuta Shrine_**. Kobe also has a handful of quality vegan restaurants like Vegan Cafe Kiu and Vegan Cafe Thallo, where you can enjoy tasty vegan lunch plates with a variety of small dishes. All of that makes Kobe an excellent day trip from Osaka or Kyoto.
