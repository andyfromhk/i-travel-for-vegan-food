Kamakura is a popular seaside city about an hour from Tokyo, making it a convenient day trip destination for vegans. The main tourist draws are the impressive **_Tsurugaoka Hachimangu shrine_** and the giant Buddha statue in **_Kotoku-in_**. However, it's worth venturing beyond the crowded sights to explore Kamakura's quieter areas. Don't miss out on the amazing vegan food in this city like the colourful sushi plates from Chirashiya, the tasty dorayaki\
from Cafe Stand Blossom Kamakura, and the epic vegan burgers at Luna Burger. While Kamakura can feel touristy at times, this city is still well\
worth visiting for its unique charm and excellent vegan food.
