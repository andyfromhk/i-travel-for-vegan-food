Wander through its many laneways decorated with bright street art, or sip an oat flat white at a trendy coffee shop, Melbourne is a city buzzing with creative energy and hidden gems waiting around every corner.

As a great spot for foodies, Melbourne boasts an incredible and well-known vegan scene. Indulge in a truly authentic Italian feast at Funghi e Tartufo, or grab some iconic, must-try fresh pastries and deli goods from the famous Smith & Deli. Melbourne's plant-based scene won't disappoint.
