This night market in West End, Brisbane has some decent vegan options. Please note that the stall availability may change from week to week.

Let's look at what you can get here:

## Location

[120 Jane St, West End QLD 4101](https://goo.gl/maps/7V1ZZbrwP8cFQnHp7)

## Hours & Date

Every Friday 4pm-midnight. Food stalls open until 10pm

## Entry Fee

Entry fee is $4 per person. Children 12 and under are free
