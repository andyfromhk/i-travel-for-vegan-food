This monthly vegan market on the Northside of Brisbane is held at VEND Marketplace (previously at UPSTAIRS area of Toombul shopping centre). You can find all kinds of delicious vegan food, fashion and other goodies here.

No matter you are craving a savoury pizza, or a sweet ice cream, you will find exactly what you want here. You may want to bring some home as well.

The entry is free and there is parking available. There are some seats at the VEND cafe area next to the market.

## Location

[1768 Sandgate Rd, Virginia QLD 4014](https://g.page/vendmarketplace?share)
