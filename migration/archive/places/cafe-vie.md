Cafe Vie closed down in February 2024.

Cafe Vie is one of the must-visit cafes on the Sunshine Coast. The menu includes some inventive cafe classics like vegan poached egg on muffin with beef, and vanilla cream red velvet pancake. The food quality is impeccable.

They also make sure you feel welcomed. Owners and staff here are warm and friendly.

They also have a good selection of cabinet baked goods including some tasty cookies and cakes. And be sure to stop by their shop section and get some goodies to take home.

One other good thing is that the owners also run an animal sanctuary called Mini Tails, and the proceeds from the cafe goes to helping the animals.
