(Tyranny of Distance is no longer vegan and now serving meat)

Tyranny of Distance in Windsor is a fully vegan bar and restaurant. The menu features mock meat-heavy, southeast-Asian inspired dishes like duck roti rolls, massaman chicken curry, as well as western comfort food like arancini and KFC burger. It has a energetic vibe and a large semi-outdoor area.
