Maple & Sage is a vegan bakery specialising in raw desserts in Maleny. It's one of the most aesthetically pleasing dessert shops I've seen. From the cakes to all the decorations, everything looks stunning!

Here you can find a selection of raw desserts, salads and smoothies. The doughnut I've tried tastes great. Other than food, their shop section carries eco-friendly household and personal care products.

A must-visit if you are near Maleny and Sunshine Coast!
