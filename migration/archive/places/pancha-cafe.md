**_Pancha has changed from vegan to serving meat since Aug 2023. This review is written prior to the change._**

Pancha is a beautiful rustic cafe in Hamilton. They put emphasis on using local produce from their family farm and from other local farmers. All the food is here vegan but they have dairy milk for coffee.

The food quality is great here with rich flavours and textures. You will find some Asian dishes like pad thai and massaman curry, both of which are delicious and authentic. If you are looking for Western flavour, their mushroom arancini is amazing. Just keep in mind that they like to put rose petals on everything.
